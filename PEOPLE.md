Seraphina Duskwhisper is a striking figure, her lithe form cloaked in an aura of quiet confidence and shadowy mystery. With raven-black hair cropped just above her shoulders and piercing green eyes that seem to catch every detail, she moves with the graceful precision of a predator. Seraphina is a survivor, raised in the shadows of bustling cities where trust was scarce, and opportunity often came at the point of a blade. For Seraphina, every step into danger is a gamble she's willing to take—because playing it safe has never been her way.

#### Backstory

Growing up as an orphan in the bustling port city of Blackwater Bay, Seraphina learned early on how to survive with quick hands and a sharp wit. She became a skilled pickpocket and con artist, working for a local thieves' guild. During one of her heists, she accidentally triggered a magical trap, awakening latent arcane abilities she didn't know she possessed. Fearing her newfound power, the guild turned on her, forcing her to flee. Now, Seraphina roams from town to town, using her arcane skills to enhance her thievery and survive in a dangerous world. She joined the party for the promise of coin but finds herself growing attached to the group, though she'd never admit it.

His ambition drives him to master his abilities and prove his worth—not just to their clan, but to the world. ## The Haverstead Family

The Haversteads are a close-knit family, doing their best to maintain the mill despite its age and the growing unease in the region.

### 1. Gregan Haverstead (Father, 44)

- **Appearance:** A broad-shouldered, weathered man with graying hair and a thick beard, usually dusted with flour.
- **Personality:**
  - *Hardworking and Gruff:* Gregan is a man of few words, focused on keeping the mill running and providing for his family.
  - *Stubborn:* He refuses to let anyone else help with the mill's upkeep, even as it becomes increasingly difficult to manage alone.
  - *Concerns:* Gregan is deeply worried about the state of the mill and the strange occurrences near Driftmoor, though he tries to keep his fears from his family.

### 2. Mara Haverstead (Mother, 40)

- **Appearance:** A kind-faced woman with auburn hair tied back in a scarf and flour smudged on her apron.
- **Personality:**
  - *Warm and Resourceful:* Mara manages the household and helps with the milling when needed, often tending to customers with a smile.
  - *Protective:* Fiercely devoted to her children, she's quick to shield them from danger or worry.

### 3. Davin Haverstead (Son, 18)

- **Appearance:** A lanky young man with a mop of dark hair and a friendly demeanor.
- **Personality:**
  - *Eager but Inexperienced:* Davin wants to help his father with the mill but lacks the skill or strength for heavy repairs.
  - *Curious:* He's fascinated by adventurers and tales of heroism, often pestering visitors with questions.

### 4. Tessa Haverstead (Daughter, 14)

- **Appearance:** A wiry, energetic girl with freckles and an infectious laugh.
- **Personality:**
  - *Cheerful and Sharp:* Tessa has a knack for fixing small problems around the mill, from patching leaks to greasing the gears.
  - *Adventurous:* She often sneaks off to explore the surrounding fields and riverbanks, despite her parents' warnings.

### 5. Baby Isla (Infant, 1)

The youngest member of the family, Isla is a chubby-cheeked baby who giggles at the sound of the grinding millstones. She's often strapped to Mara's back as she works.
