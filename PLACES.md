# Places

## The Region Around Driftmoor

### 1. Driftmoor Town

Perched on a rocky coast where the Greywater River flows into the Azure Sea, Driftmoor is nestled between jagged cliffs and windswept plains, giving it an isolated but picturesque setting. Its population is ~500 (mostly humans, halflings, and a few dwarves and elves).

**The Harbor District:** A small harbor sheltered by rocky outcroppings. Wooden piers and fishing boats dominate the scene, with ramshackle homes belonging to the fisherfolk. The waters are teeming with fish, but the occasional shadowy form beneath the surface unnerves the locals.

**The Village Center:** The heart of Driftmoor, where modest stone and timber buildings house shops, a tavern (The Broken Lantern), and the mayor's hall.

**Farms and Fields:** A mile upriver are scattered farms growing hardy grains, root vegetables, and some grazing livestock. Farmers often transport their goods downstream to the harbor by raft.

---

### 2. The Obelisk Reach

A jagged expanse of cliffs and rocky plateaus north of Driftmoor, named for the shattered obelisk that looms over the landscape. The terrain is treacherous, with narrow paths, hidden caves, and eerie rock formations. The obelisk's ruins are the source of local legends and the shadowbeasts plaguing the area. Strange symbols carved into the cliffs are thought to predate even the oldest civilizations.

**The Shattered Obelisk:** A towering, fractured stone spire that hums faintly with magical energy. Its fragments are scattered across the cliffs, some embedded deep in the rock.

**Echoing Caverns:** A network of caves beneath the Reach, said to lead to ancient chambers. Unnatural sounds often echo from within.

---

### 3. The Greywater River

A fast-moving river that cuts through the region, originating in the Mistwood Hills to the northeast and flowing into the sea at Driftmoor. The river is vital for both irrigation and transportation. It is home to trout, otters, and kingfishers, but strange, shadowy creatures have been seen skulking near its banks in recent months.

**The Old Mill:** A crumbling stone mill a few miles upriver run by a small family and active in the fall during harvest.

**Willowford Bridge:** A sturdy wooden bridge crossing the river, often watched by a farmer who charges a modest toll.

---

### 4. The Mistwood Hills

Rolling hills covered in dense, fog-laden forests that stretch northeast of Driftmoor. The Mistwood is known for its eerie atmosphere and towering pines. Hunters and loggers speak of vanishing paths, strange whispers, and shadowy figures deeper in the woods. Few venture far without good reason.

**The Old Watchtower:** A crumbling stone tower on a hilltop, once used to spot incoming raiders. It's now overgrown and abandoned, but it offers a clear view of the region.

**Fey Glade:** Hidden deep in the Mistwood, this glade is said to shimmer with faint light and strange, otherworldly energy.

---

### 5. The Azure Sea

A cold, rocky coastline with strong tides and unpredictable weather. Driftmoor's harbor is one of the few safe spots for fishing boats and trade vessels in the area.

**Sea Stacks:** Tall, isolated rock formations rising from the water near the shore. Legends say the sea stacks were once giants turned to stone.

**The Drowned Isle:** A partially submerged island visible only during low tide. Fisherfolk claim it's cursed, and shadowy shapes have been spotted around it at night.

---

### 6. The Plains of Stonehearth

Windswept grasslands dotted with rocky outcroppings and small caves, located southeast of Driftmoor. These plains are used for grazing sheep and goats but are sparsely populated. Shadowbeasts have been sighted here, preying on livestock and unwary travelers.

---

### Environmental Challenges

**Unpredictable Weather:** Coastal storms roll in quickly, bringing heavy rain and strong winds that can make travel dangerous.

**Treacherous Terrain:** The cliffs of the Obelisk Reach and the sea stacks near the coast are perilous, with crumbling edges and slippery surfaces.

**Wildlife and Threats:** The region is home to wolves, giant crabs near the coast, and now, shadowbeasts.

---

### Adventure Opportunities

1. **Driftmoor and the Harbor:** Smugglers are rumored to operate near the harbor, and Kelem Dustcloak might have dealings with them.
2. **The Greywater River:** A farmer's child has gone missing near the Old Mill, and shadowy figures have been spotted in the area.
3. **Obelisk Reach:** Strange lights and shadowy creatures have been reported near the shattered obelisk, drawing the party into the mystery.
4. **Mistwood Hills:** Villagers claim to hear haunting music and see faint lights in the woods—could it be fey magic or something darker?
5. **The Drowned Isle:** A local fisherman found an ancient, barnacle-covered chest on the island but was too afraid to open it.

## Driftmoor Town

### The Mayor's Hall

*Aldren Thorne (Human, Male)*

The Mayor's Hall is one of the largest and most prominent buildings in Driftmoor, reflecting its importance as the center of local governance. Situated near the village square, the hall is a sturdy, two-story structure built from timber and stone, designed to withstand the coastal weather and occasional storms that batter the region. Though practical in design, small touches—such as carved wooden trim along the eaves and a neatly kept flower bed outside—show that Driftmoor takes quiet pride in its leadership.

**Exterior:**
The front facade features wide double doors made of dark oak, reinforced with iron bands and decorated with a simple carving of Driftmoor's crest: a fish and a sheaf of grain crossed beneath a rising sun. A flagpole stands nearby, bearing a faded banner in similar colors. A short set of stone steps leads up to the entrance, flanked by lanterns that are lit each evening.

**Interior:**

*Main Hall:* The heart of the building is a spacious room where the mayor addresses the townsfolk or holds council meetings. Rows of simple wooden benches face a raised dais with a long table and a high-backed chair for the mayor. The walls are adorned with maps of the surrounding region, fishing nets, and a few framed sketches of Driftmoor from its earlier days. A weathered bronze bell hangs near the back of the room, used to call meetings or sound an alarm.

*Mayor's Office:* To the left of the main hall, a smaller room serves as Mayor Aldren Thorne's office. The space is cluttered with piles of papers, ledgers, and correspondence. A large desk dominates the room, and behind it is a shelf holding dusty legal texts and a small collection of curios from Driftmoor's history. A bottle of whiskey and a pair of glasses are discreetly tucked in one corner. A window looks out toward the harbor.

*Records Room:* Behind the office is a small, locked room containing records of town business, property deeds, and local laws. The room is meticulously organized, thanks to the mayor's sharp-eyed clerk, Elise Harper.

*Upstairs Living Quarters:* Mayor Thorne lives above the hall in a modest suite of rooms. His bedroom is sparse but comfortable, with a sturdy bed, a small writing desk, and a trunk containing a few personal belongings.

**Adventure Hooks:**
1. *A Missing Shipment:* Aldren hires the party to investigate a missing shipment of supplies intended for the town, suspecting bandits or worse.
2. *A Dispute:* A land dispute between two local farmers escalates, and Aldren asks the party to mediate before it turns violent.
3. *Records of the Past:* Aldren mentions an old town ledger in the records room that references strange incidents near the obelisk decades ago, but it's gone missing.

### Elyssa Moonglow's Residence

*Elyssa Moonglow (High Elf, Female)*

Elyssa Moonglow's residence is a small, cluttered cottage on the outskirts of Driftmoor, perched on a low hill overlooking the Greywater River. Part library, part laboratory, and part living space, the cottage reflects Elyssa's dual identity as a scholar and historian.

**Exterior:**
The cottage is a simple stone building with a slate roof and ivy creeping up its walls. A small chimney emits thin trails of smoke. The path to the door is lined with overgrown lavender and rosemary bushes, and a small herb garden sits to the side. A wooden sign by the door reads "E. Moonglow – Researcher" in elegant Elvish script.

**Interior:**

*Main Room:* The largest room serves as both study and library. Shelves line every available wall, overflowing with books, scrolls, and dusty tomes. A large wooden desk near the window is cluttered with quills, ink bottles, and half-unrolled maps of Driftmoor and the surrounding region.

*The Laboratory:* A smaller side room dedicated to arcane studies, containing a worn alchemical workbench, a faintly glowing obelisk shard encased in a reinforced glass dome surrounded by chalk glyphs, and crates of excavation tools and partially cleaned artifacts.

*The Bedroom:* Small and sparsely furnished, with only a narrow bed, a wardrobe, and a table holding a single candle. Elyssa often falls asleep at her desk instead.

**Notable Items:**
- *Research Notes:* Documents detailing the history of the obelisk, its potential origins, and its current effects on the region.
- *Obelisk Fragment:* A shard of the shattered obelisk stored in her laboratory, faintly pulsing with dark energy.
- *Ancient Maps:* Several old, hand-drawn maps showing the region around Driftmoor, with the obelisk marked as a central point.
- *Rare Artifacts:* Pieces of pottery, carved stone glyphs, and rusted tools from an unknown civilization.

**Adventure Hooks:**
1. *Unfinished Work:* Elyssa hires the party to retrieve more obelisk shards or explore ruins near the obelisk for additional artifacts.
2. *Mysterious Visitor:* Elyssa mentions that a cloaked figure recently visited her cottage, asking strange questions about the obelisk before disappearing.
3. *Sabotage:* Elyssa discovers her laboratory ransacked, with the obelisk fragment stolen. She begs the party to recover it before it falls into the wrong hands.

### The Mercantile: "Salt & Iron"

*Jorgen Hallowtide (Human, Male)*

The "Salt & Iron Mercantile" is Driftmoor's primary trading post, providing adventurers, farmers, and fisherfolk with essential supplies and the occasional luxury. Run by the no-nonsense Jorgen Hallowtide, the shop stocks a wide variety of practical goods, tools, and imported wares.

**The Shop:**
Located in the center of Driftmoor, near the village square. Made of timber reinforced with iron bands. A swinging wooden sign depicting a sack of salt and crossed iron tongs hangs above the double doors. The interior is organized but crowded, with shelves running along the walls and barrels stacked in the corners. A long counter dominates the center, with Jorgen usually found behind it. A small upstairs area serves as his private living quarters.

**Services:**
- *Basic Repairs:* Non-magical equipment (weapons, armor, tools) for 2–5 gp depending on damage.
- *Appraisal:* Approximate value of trade goods or treasures for 1 gp per item.
- *Supplies on Credit:* Extended to trusted individuals; failure to repay earns his ire—and possibly a bounty.

**Adventure Hooks:**
1. *A Missing Caravan:* One of Jorgen's supply caravans never arrived. He hires the party to investigate, suspecting shadowbeasts or bandits.
2. *Stolen Goods:* Jorgen suspects someone is smuggling exotic items out of his store and offers a reward to catch the thief.
3. *A Rare Item:* Jorgen has heard rumors of an artifact hidden in the Echoing Caverns and offers a generous reward for its retrieval.
4. *Merchant Rivalry:* Jorgen believes Kelem Dustcloak is undercutting him with stolen or illicit goods and might hire the party to investigate—or sabotage—Kelem's operations.

### The Apothecary: "The Bitter Remedy"

*Lytha Varrow (Half-Elf, Female)*

"The Bitter Remedy" is a modest yet well-stocked apothecary serving both locals and occasional adventurers. Known for its potent herbal remedies and unorthodox cures, as well as a few questionable concoctions with mysterious side effects.

**The Shop:**
Nestled near the edge of the village center, not far from the Greywater River. A small, weathered stone structure with a crooked chimney and green vines creeping up the walls. Shelves lining the walls are crammed with jars, vials, and bundles of dried herbs. The air is heavy with the scent of lavender, mint, and something faintly bitter. A wooden counter in the center is covered in mortar-and-pestle tools, scales, and open tomes. A small back room holds Lytha's personal experiments and more volatile ingredients.

**Services:**
- *Custom Remedies:* Crafted in 1–2 days given proper ingredients.
- *Herbal Knowledge:* Insight into plants and alchemical ingredients for a fee or a favor.
- *Monster Part Appraisal:* Identification of the alchemical properties of monster parts.

**Adventure Hooks:**
1. *Missing Apprentice:* Lytha's apprentice Fennel went into the Mistwood Hills to collect herbs but hasn't returned.
2. *Rare Ingredient:* Lytha needs Moonlace, a rare herb that only grows near the shattered obelisk, and offers a hefty reward.
3. *Unstable Experiment:* One of Lytha's potions has gone wrong, creating a living mass of writhing vines that has taken over her back room.
4. *Shadowbeast Cure:* Lytha believes she can brew a potion to repel shadowbeasts but needs fresh blood from one of the creatures.

### The Church: "The Beacon"

*Father Elden Greybeard (Human, Male) | Mira (Human, Female, Acolyte)*

"The Beacon" is a small but sturdy stone church dedicated to Pelor, God of Light and Healing. It serves as a place of worship, refuge, and community gathering for Driftmoor's residents.

**The Chapel:**
Situated on a small rise overlooking the village, with a wooden steeple. At night, its lantern is lit as a symbol of hope for the town. The inside is simple but well-kept, with wooden pews, a stone altar adorned with symbols of the sun, and tall narrow windows. A small garden behind the church grows medicinal herbs and flowers.

**Services:**
- *Basic Healing:* 5 gp donation.
- *Greater Healing or Curing Disease:* 25 gp donation.
- *Refuge:* Sanctuary to anyone in need, though space is limited.
- *Herbs and Remedies:* Shared freely or for a modest donation.

**Adventure Hooks:**
1. *The Lantern's Glow:* The church's nightly lantern has begun to flicker with a strange, dark light. Elden is deeply troubled and asks the party to uncover the cause.
2. *Mira's Calling:* Mira seeks the party's help in retrieving an ancient holy relic from the Mistwood Hills, believing it might protect Driftmoor from the growing darkness.
3. *Elden's Past:* Elden reveals that he once ventured into the Echoing Caverns as a young cleric, but fled when his companions were lost. He urges the party to retrieve their remains and put their spirits to rest.

The Broken Lantern is Driftmoor's only tavern, a lively gathering spot for locals, fishermen, and the occasional traveler. *Bar Counter:* A long, polished wooden bar runs along one side, lined with stools. Behind it, shelves hold an assortment of bottles, casks, and jars filled with questionable concoctions.

*Stage Corner:* A small raised platform used for performances or announcements, currently stacked with empty crates.

*Private Booths:* A few shadowy booths line the walls, favored by those seeking privacy.

6. *Lost Treasure:* Tess mentions an old rumor about a previous owner hiding a valuable relic in the storage room.
7. *Kidnapped Guest:* A guest staying upstairs has gone missing, their room left in disarray, with a cryptic note mentioning the obelisk.

## The Old Mill

*The Haverstead Family*

The Old Mill, located on the Greywater River a few miles upriver from Driftmoor, is an essential part of the local economy, grinding grain into flour for the townsfolk and surrounding farms. Despite its importance, the mill is a crumbling relic of a bygone era, with weathered stone walls, sagging beams, and a wheel that creaks ominously with each turn.

**The Mill and Surroundings:**

*Structure:* A large, two-story stone building with a steep, moss-covered roof. Cracks spiderweb across its exterior, and ivy climbs up the walls. The waterwheel is massive but old, its wooden paddles worn thin by years of use.

*Ground Floor:* The main grinding area is dominated by a pair of large millstones. The room is filled with the constant rumble of grinding grain, the sweet scent of flour, and a fine layer of dust coating every surface. Sacks of grain are stacked high in one corner.

*Upper Floor:* The family's modest living quarters, with a communal sleeping area and a small kitchen. The roof leaks in heavy rain, and the walls are patched with mismatched wood and plaster.

*Surroundings:* A small yard where farmers unload their grain. Beyond that, the riverbank is lined with willows, and fields stretch out in every direction, golden and freshly harvested.

**The Mill in Fall:**
With the harvest in full swing, the Haversteads are busier than ever. Farmers arrive daily to drop off grain and pick up flour. Gregan works long hours to keep the millstones turning, while Mara and the children help with sorting, bagging, and cleaning. The mill's age is starting to show—the waterwheel groans with each rotation, and the grinding stones occasionally seize up.

**Adventure Hooks:**
1. *Structural Emergency:* Heavy rains have eroded the riverbank, threatening to collapse part of the mill. The Haversteads desperately need help shoring it up.
2. *Missing Daughter:* Tessa hasn't returned from one of her explorations near the obelisk, and the family fears she's in danger.
3. *Sabotage:* Strange symbols are found carved into the mill's beams, and Gregan suspects someone—or something—is trying to ruin their livelihood.
4. *Shadow Attack:* A shadowbeast attacks one of the farmers delivering grain, prompting the family to seek protection from adventurers.
