### clawed_tracks

A fading trail of clawed prints crosses the packed earth near the stable
entrance. Each mark has three narrow forward claws and a broader rear pad,
pressed more deeply than the tracks of a fox or stray dog. Scuffed hoofprints
and days of stable traffic have broken the trail in several places, but the
clearest impressions point out toward the Mistwood road rather than back
toward town.

The tracks begin near the empty stall of the missing prize horse. There are no
matching hoofprints returning to the stall, and the disturbed earth suggests
something heavy was dragged or led away. Anyone who has examined shadowbeast
tracks elsewhere recognizes the same print shape; otherwise the animal that
made them cannot be identified from these fading marks alone.

*(Not pickup-able.)*

*Size:* medium

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine / inspect / study** → The clearest print shows three long forward claws around a broad rear pad. It is too large and deep for an ordinary fox or dog, though age and stable traffic make a precise identification impossible.
- **search / investigate** → The surviving prints begin beside the empty stall and lead toward the Mistwood road. No matching horse tracks return, and a shallow furrow between two prints suggests that something heavy passed this way.
- **follow / track** → You can follow the broken trail out toward the Mistwood road, but the marks fade on the harder ground beyond the stable. Their direction clearly points toward Mistwood Forest.
- **touch / measure** → The best print is roughly the spread of an adult hand. Its edges crumble when touched, confirming that it is days or weeks old rather than fresh.
- **compare** → Anyone who has previously examined shadowbeast tracks recognizes the same three-clawed shape and broad pad. Without such a reference, the resemblance remains only an unsettling possibility.
