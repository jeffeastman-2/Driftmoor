## Description and Atmosphere
Cramped, low-ceilinged. Barrels of ale, sacks of grain, crates of dried goods stacked to the rafters. Smells of wood, yeast, and old food. A single lamp on a hook. It is strictly a supply room and too cramped to serve as a meeting space.

Tess has a hidden compartment under one of the floorboards. Only she knows about it.
