## Items

### ledger_book
A heavy bound ledger sits near one edge of the round table with pages of room tabs, supply costs, and fish-delivery payments. Recent entries have corrections in two different inks.

*(Pickup-able.)*

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine** → Reveals disputed payout lines and room-rental records from the last week.
- **pick up / take** → Portable but conspicuous; removing it escalates social conflict quickly.

### sealed_letter
A folded letter stamped with dark wax rests under the edge of the ledger. The seal shows no family crest, only a plain circular press.

*(Pickup-able.)*

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine** → The addressee line references a late-night meeting in this room.
- **open / unseal** → Breaking the wax reveals a terse note about a delivery route and concern about "north-road witnesses."
- **read / decipher** → The note directs an unnamed courier to change the delivery route and warns that "north-road witnesses" must be dealt with before the next meeting.

## Notes for DM
- The parchment left behind is the evidence thread connecting the meeting room to whatever the suspicious travelers are doing
- Contents should connect to either the harbor smuggler plotline or the obelisk shard trade
