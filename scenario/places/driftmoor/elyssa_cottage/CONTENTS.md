# CONTENTS.md — Elyssa's Cottage (General)

## Items Present (Herb Garden)
- Lavender, rosemary (overgrown)
- Medicinal herbs (functional but less organized than church garden)

## Notes for DM
- The herb garden is a minor resource — Lytha Varrow occasionally trades with Elyssa for specimens

## Study
- Extensive library of history, arcane theory, and regional geography
- Obelisk research notes and ancient maps of the region
- Pottery shards, carved stone glyphs, and rusted tools of unknown design

Elyssa shares the notes with serious allies once she trusts them. A DC 13
Investigation check on the oldest map reveals a symbol matching marks appearing
on Driftmoor buildings and the mill.

## Laboratory
- Alchemical workbench, excavation tools, and partially cleaned artifacts
- **Obelisk Shard** under a reinforced glass dome and chalk containment glyphs
- Research notes specific to the shard's behavior

If the ransacking event triggers, the dome is broken, the shard is missing,
and scattered notes can be reconstructed with a DC 11 Investigation check.
