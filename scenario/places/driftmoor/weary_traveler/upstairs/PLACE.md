## Description and Atmosphere
A broad plank-floored hall runs across the upper story beneath dark roof beams. Three small but clean guest rooms stand in a row along its north side, each with a numbered door and a simple latch. Floorboards carry every heavy footstep, while the smells of woodsmoke and lamb stew rise from the common room below.

The southeast stair returns to the common room. A linen cupboard near the opposite end of the hall holds folded sheets, towels, and spare blankets.
