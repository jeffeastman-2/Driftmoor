# PLACE.md — Fisherfolk Quarter

## Place Identity
- **type:** residential quarter
- **aliases:** fisherfolk, fisherfolk quarter, fishing quarter, fishermen's homes, harbor homes

## Description and Atmosphere
A close row of salt-weathered cottages shares a hard-packed work yard above the
docks. Nets hang from pegs and lines, lobster pots are stacked beneath the
eaves, and patched oilskins drip beside doors painted in fading blues and
greens. This is a lived-in neighborhood rather than a shop: families mend gear,
sort the morning's catch, and watch strangers with frank harbor curiosity.
