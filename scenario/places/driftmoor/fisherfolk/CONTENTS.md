# CONTENTS.md — Fisherfolk Quarter

## Items

### communal_net_racks
Long racks hold drying nets in several stages of repair. Cork floats, stone
weights, and bone needles sit in shallow work baskets beneath them.

*(Not pickup-able; flavor.)*

### stacked_lobster_pots
Creels and lobster pots are stacked against a cottage wall, each marked with a
family stripe in faded paint.

*(Not pickup-able; flavor.)*

## Notes for DM
- Treat the quarter as a residential common yard, not a store.
- Unnamed neighbors may provide harbor atmosphere, but should not become
  indistinct creature tokens or acquire invented authority over town events.
