## Description and Atmosphere
An old walled burial ground on the rise west of the Beacon. Salt-stunted grass
grows between leaning stones, and the oldest inscriptions have been worn nearly
smooth by coastal weather. A narrow iron gate opens from the town path.

No hostile creature permanently occupies the graveyard. Crows, insects, and
other ordinary wildlife are ambient rather than active scene participants.
