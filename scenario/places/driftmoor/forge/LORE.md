# LORE.md — The Ironhoof Forge Marks
## DM Eyes Only

---

## What Happened

The marks were not carved by a person and are not deliberate vandalism. They
formed when a pulse from the damaged obelisk network found a temporary path
through the forge: iron stock, the hearth, buried mineral seams, and repeated
hammering made the building an accidental resonator. The pulse heated the iron
but pulled heat out of the stone, leaving the grooves cold and blackened.

Darrin first noticed them less than a week ago. They have spread by several
hair-thin branches since then, always after dusk and always toward the hearth.
His attempts to scour and chisel them away removed ordinary soot and a shallow
layer of stone, but exposed the same pattern underneath.

## Relationship to the Obelisk Mystery

The hooked three-line motif belongs to the same symbolic system as the glyphs
in Elyssa's research and the disturbances associated with shadowbeasts. Here it
describes transmission rather than containment: energy entered through a wider
network, converged in the forge wall, and continued toward a conductive focus.

The forge is not itself a portal. It is evidence that the damaged network can
use ordinary structures as conduits when their materials and repeated activity
create the right resonance. This makes the marks an early warning, not merely
a clue from an event already finished.

## What Examination Can Establish

- **Anyone:** The lines are structured, unnaturally cold, and formed within the
  stone. They cannot be explained as soot, cracking, tool work, or an ordinary
  burn.
- **A smith or mason:** The wall was sound before the marks appeared. Neither
  forge heat nor thermal stress produces inward-fused grooves like these.
- **Someone familiar with Elyssa's maps or obelisk glyphs:** The hooked motif
  and branching grammar match, but their orientation indicates flow toward the
  hearth rather than a ward or boundary.
- **Arcane examination:** The residue holds a weak, fading signature combining
  transmutation and shadow influence. It is trace evidence, not an active spell.

## Possible Follow-ups

A clean residue sample gives Elyssa physical evidence that the network is
reaching into Driftmoor. Comparing it with her charcoal maps lets her identify
the marks as a conduit diagram and narrows the pulse's likely origin toward the
Obelisk Reach. Heating or hammering iron near the wall after dusk may cause the
finest veins to darken briefly, confirming that forge activity strengthens the
resonance.

The marks should not attack, possess, or open a portal during an ordinary first
investigation. Their threat is implication: if the network can recruit a forge,
other structures in town may already be participating unnoticed.
