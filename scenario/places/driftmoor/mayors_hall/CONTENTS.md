# CONTENTS.md — The Mayor's Hall (Main Hall)

## Items Present

### Mundane
- Rows of simple wooden benches
- Long table and high-backed chair on raised dais
- Maps of the surrounding region (general, not detailed)
- Fishing nets (decorative)
- Framed sketches of early Driftmoor

### Notable
- Weathered bronze bell — functional alarm and meeting bell
- Town crest carving on the main doors

## Creatures
- None normally

## Notes for DM
- The bronze bell can be used as an alarm — ringing it will bring townsfolk
- The regional maps on the walls are rough but could orient players early in the session

## Mayor's Office
- Large desk, official correspondence, legal texts, and historical curios
- Discreet bottle of whiskey
- Aldren's guarded notes on the shadowbeast situation

## Records Room
- Bound property deeds, local laws, census records, and historical ledgers
- **Missing:** the old ledger concerning incidents near the obelisk
- An unmarked early obelisk ledger on the bottom shelf (DC 12 Investigation)

The room is locked (DC 15 Thieves' Tools); Elise and Aldren carry keys. Elise
will not leave visitors alone here.

## Living Quarters
- Sturdy bed, writing desk, personal correspondence, and a locked trunk
- Aldren's private journal and an unlabeled portrait

The trunk uses a simple lock (DC 10 Thieves' Tools) or Aldren's key. The
portrait's identity remains at the DM's discretion.
