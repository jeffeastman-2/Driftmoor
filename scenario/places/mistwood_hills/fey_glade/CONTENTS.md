# CONTENTS.md — The Fey Glade

## Creatures
- Shadowbeast: at TREELINE (outer, TRAIL-facing side). Patrolling the
  perimeter for three days. Cannot cross the repulsion boundary into the
  GLADE — at dusk the boundary inflicts 1d4 radiant damage and requires a
  DC 8 Wisdom save to cross; at night, DC 18 and 1d6+2 damage. See
  `GEOMETRY.md` (Boundaries).
- Fey: level of engagement is DM's discretion — from passive shimmer to
  active NPC encounter.

## Items

### shadowbeast_tracks
Cloven prints in the soft earth of the trail, leading north toward the
glade. The tracks deepen and grow more agitated as they approach the
treeline, then go cold at the TREELINE itself — as if the creature paced
the boundary but did not cross it. *(Not pickup-able; flavor.)*

### dense_cover
Tangled ferns, low boughs, and snarls of root along the forest edge.
A creature behind this cover has partial concealment (light obscurement)
against ranged attacks from the glade or trail. *(Not pickup-able;
flavor.)*

### fey_flowers
Out-of-season flowers scattered through the glade in small clusters —
pale blue and white, blooming in summer when they should not. A character
trained in Nature recognizes them as medicinal (DC 13 Nature) with mild
restorative properties when steeped. *(Pickup-able.)*

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine** → The flowers are healthy and unwilted despite the late
  hour. Their presence in this season is itself evidence the glade is
  not behaving like ordinary Mistwood forest.
- **pick / harvest** → A handful comes away easily. Yield enough to brew
  three doses of a mild healing draught (1d4 HP each) if prepared by
  someone with herbalism.
- **identify** (DC 13 Nature) → The character names the species and the
  out-of-season bloom as a marker of fey influence.

### fey_herbs
Fey-touched variants of common forest herbs — sage, wormwort, bittergreen
— growing thick along the pool's edge. They look ordinary at a glance but
hum faintly to the touch. *(Pickup-able; quest item for Lytha Varrow; she
pays double the standard rate for fey-touched ingredients, and they double
the efficacy of any potion she brews with them.)*

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine** → A close look shows the leaves are subtly wrong: veins
  too bright, edges too even. They are common herbs that have been
  *touched* — the same plants that grow throughout the Mistwood, but
  charged.
- **harvest** → A handful comes away easily.

### clear_spring
The shallow pool in the center of the glade. The water is pure, faintly
cool, and slightly restorative — a creature that drinks deeply recovers
1 HP. The surface is glass-still even when wind stirs the trees above.

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine** → The bed is pale stone, visible through perhaps two feet
  of water. No fish, no insects. The stillness is unnatural but not
  threatening.
- **drink** → Restores 1 HP. The effect does not stack across rests; a
  given character may benefit only once per day.
- **fill waterskin** → Holds its potency for one day, then becomes
  ordinary water.

### carved_stone
A flat, half-buried stone at the water's edge, its upper face worked with
shallow spiraling grooves. The carvings are old enough that lichen has
filled them in places, but the pattern is unmistakably deliberate. The
stone hums faintly when touched, in time with the pool's stillness.
*(Not pickup-able; pressable.)*

*Interactions (DM ground truth):*
- **examine** → The grooves form a spiral that begins at the stone's
  center and unwinds outward toward the pool. A character who has seen
  fey-work recognizes the pattern as a binding or focus — not a
  containment, but an *anchor*.
- **press / lay hands on** → The hum deepens briefly and the pool's
  surface ripples outward from the stone, though no wind touches it.
  Nothing else visible happens. The repulsion boundary on the glade
  pulses perceptibly for one round.
- **pry up / dig out** → The stone resists. It is set deeper than its
  exposed face suggests, and the surrounding earth is laced with roots
  that have grown *into* the carvings. A character can dig for 10
  minutes and not reach its base.

### ground_shimmer
Near the center of the glade, a slow shimmer rises from the ground — a
soft, sourceless light that drifts up from the moss and dissipates a few
feet above the earth. It does not illuminate; it is the visible signature
of the fey resonance that keeps the shadowbeast at bay. *(Not
pickup-able; flavor.)*

## Notes for DM
- The glade is a sanctuary and a resource — the party should feel it as
  a genuinely safe place, which makes it valuable.
- Time distortion: anyone who sleeps in the glade wakes feeling fully
  rested regardless of actual time passed, but may have missed events.
- The `fey_herbs` double Lytha Varrow's potion efficacy if used as
  ingredients (and she pays double for them).
- Fennel cannot call out — sound brings the shadowbeast closer.
- Fennel has 3 arrows left. His only other weapon is the improvised spear.
  He knows this.
- A "fey gift" may appear in the clearing at the DM's discretion: a
  small object of unexpected value, with an attached obligation. Not
  modeled as an Item — narrate into being if the moment calls for it.
- Item ids in this file match the `## Items` table in `GEOMETRY.md`.
  Add new items to both files together.
