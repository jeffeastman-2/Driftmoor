# Pool Cavern

## Description
A long low cavern dominated by still black water that occupies most of the floor. Torchlight skates across the surface but returns little, as if the pool resents giving anything back.

Beyond it, a collapsed tunnel seals the obvious route while implying that the real path continues out of sight.
