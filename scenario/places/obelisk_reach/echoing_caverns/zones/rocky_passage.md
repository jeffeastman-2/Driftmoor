# Rocky Passage

## Description
A narrow worked corridor extending west from Echo Chamber. Unlike the smoother approach caves, these walls are cut with deliberate lines and old chisel certainty. Dust-grey web strands cross at face height and cling to armor, cloak hems, and torch shafts.

This passage feels inhabited by absence: no fresh tracks, no loose rubble, only the sense that anything entering is already expected.
