# First Passage

## Description

A short downward passage of damp, dark stone, sloping noticeably underfoot. The walls narrow enough that two cannot easily walk abreast—a corridor carved with deliberate purpose, not the smooth winding of water-worn cave. Daylight from the entrance above fades here to absence; beyond the reach of even a bright torch, the ceiling vanishes into shadow.

The sounds from deeper in grow more distinct here without ever quite resolving into something nameable. Not wind, not water, not quite voice—something in between, almost rhythmic, as though the stone itself were breathing. Footsteps carry strangely: sometimes ahead of you, sometimes behind, sometimes doubled as though there were more of you than there are. Your own echoes betray you here.

The air is colder than the chamber above and carries a faint taste of minerals and old depth. This passage is a threshold—the last place where the world above feels real. Past it lies something else.

## Ambient

- **Light:** None. Beyond torchlight and darkvision, absolute darkness. The passage is steep enough that shadows pool in the descending curve, making distance hard to judge.
- **Sound:** Profoundly strange. Footsteps carry at odd angles—sometimes preceding the person who made them, sometimes lagging. Your own breathing returns from ahead of you before you finish exhaling. The deeper hum from the Echo Chamber resolves slightly here, almost like a vast bell struck once a moment ago and still vibrating. The unnatural echo boundary makes sound localization nearly impossible.
- **Temperature:** Noticeably colder than the Entry Chamber. The chill is damp, soaking into clothes. Breath mists faintly.
- **Footing:** Steep descent. The stone is worn smooth in the center from passage, but treacherous at the edges where ancient water-wear has left irregular, jagged rock. More than one companion may have fallen here in the dark.
- **Smell:** Mineral-sharp. Wet stone. The faint copper-iron trace of very old blood, barely perceptible but unmistakable once noticed. Something else: a smell like metal left too long in damp—rust-deep, wrong.

## Features / Landmarks

- **first_passage_mouth** — The northern threshold, where the floor of the Entry Chamber sinks into this passage. A natural fold of stone narrows the opening; no taller than a stooped man, two cannot walk abreast.
- **echo_chamber_arch** — The southern end of the passage, where the walls fall away and the Echo Chamber's vault opens overhead beyond torchlight. The sound changes here perceptibly: a beat too late, slightly altered, as though the chamber itself were repeating what the passage said back to it *wrong*.

## Creatures Present

None. The passage is empty, but evidence from above suggests something moved through here recently—and something may be listening from below.

### Creatures (≤ 2 per zone)

- **Echoing Shade** — A fragment of the obelisk's influence made mobile—not alive in any conventional sense, but aware. It lingers in the First Passage, drawn to sound and movement. Power: minor. Role: haunter / herald. Fits because: The passage is explicitly described as a place where sound is distorted and unnatural; an encounter that *is* that distortion would ground the supernatural wrongness the party has already sensed. It would not attack but would amplify and mock the party's own sounds back at them, creating disorientation. Its presence would confirm that something in these caverns is actively hostile and aware.

- **Stone Shard Swarm** — A cluster of fractured obelisk fragments suspended in the passage's draft, moving with almost-life. Power: minor. Role: environmental hazard. Fits because: The Entry Chamber contains scattered obelisk fragments and shard evidence; a swarm of smaller shards in transition would tie the scattered debris to the larger pattern and create a navigation challenge (darkvision works through them, but light-based perception is confused by the scatter and reflections). The fragments hum faintly, contributing to the passage's unnatural acoustic.

### Treasure & Notable Items (≤ 3 per zone)

- **Cracked Obelisk Shard** — A fragment of the inscribed stone, half the size of a fist, worked loose from the walls over decades and lodged in a crevice in the passage floor. The script on one face is clear and undamaged; the other is fractured. Tier: mundane. ~Value: curiosity; invaluable to Elyssa for linguistic study. Affordances: pickable, small, weight ~0.5 lb. Fits because: The passage's walls, though not explicitly carved with script like the Echo Chamber's, are part of the obelisk system; a fragment here connects the entry mysteries to what the deeper inscriptions reveal. It would be a concrete link for Elyssa's research and reward careful observation.

- **Worn Leather Glove** — A single work glove, fingers intact but palm worn through, caught in a crack beside one of the passage walls. Animal-leather, aged and stiff. Fits because: It matches the evidence trail from the Entry Chamber (discarded pack, bloodstain, drag marks). Finding it confirms someone made it at least this far through the passage, and the wear pattern suggests a second survivor—the one doing the dragging. The glove is leather-only, no ornament, matching the journeyman expedition tone established in the Entry Chamber.

### Features (optional)

- **Hairline crack running the passage length** — The western wall shows a very faint vertical seam where the stone fit poorly when hewn, now made slightly visible by centuries of damp seepage and mineral deposit. At intermediate distance it reads as a slight discoloration; the crack itself is too narrow for entry. Fits because: It suggests the passage was deliberately carved (not natural water-wear) and has stood a very long time—adding to the sense of geological patience and age.

- **Draft from below** — A faint but unmistakable current of air rising from the Echo Chamber below, cold and carrying that mineral-sharp smell. Characters sensitive to air movement (or standing still long enough) notice the draft shifts slightly every few seconds, as though something deep in the caverns were breathing.
