# First Passage

## Description
This short descent links Entry Chamber to Echo Chamber. The walls are narrow enough that two people cannot comfortably walk abreast. Daylight dies out almost immediately, and the passage feels carved for movement rather than habitation.

Sounds from below become clearer here while staying impossible to place. Footsteps can seem to come from ahead, then behind. It is a threshold zone where the natural cave gives way to the deeper, wrong acoustics of the scene.
