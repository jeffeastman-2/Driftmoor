# Deep Passage

## Description
A steep dry descent from Echo Chamber into the lower west pocket of the cave system. Carvings stop at the threshold as if language itself gave up here, leaving only plain stone and old dust.

Every step downward strips away the upper chamber's noise until only breath and boot scrape remain.
