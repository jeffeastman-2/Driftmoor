# Mirror Approach

## Description
A narrow gallery bends south toward a side entrance to the Hall. Its paired
silver mirrors show movements a fraction before they happen: a lifted hand,
then the hand lifting; a glance over the shoulder, then the impulse to look.
They preview the Hall's more dangerous visions and allow a cautious party to
enter beside, rather than directly before, the eight dark mirrors.
