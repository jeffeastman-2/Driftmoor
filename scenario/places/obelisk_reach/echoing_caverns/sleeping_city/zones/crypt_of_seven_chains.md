# Crypt of Seven Chains

## Description
The doors do not stand open so much as hesitate. They grind inward by the width
of a finger, stop, and drift apart again. Beyond them, seven pillars surround a
bier of black stone. Six chains cross the chamber, bright as moonlight beneath
water; four are sound and two visibly frayed. The seventh lies broken on the
floor. Vaelithra rests beneath them in ruined royal silks, ancient and
desiccated, her jeweled throat untouched by dust. Her eyes open when Kaelin
enters. Far beyond the walls, thousands of sleepers take the same first breath.

The crypt, not Vaelithra, takes initiative. Closure requires three simultaneous
functions: present a sympathetic link to the living lock, sound the unresolved
key melody, and turn the prison toward closure. Run three ritual rounds, each
requiring at least three meaningful actions at different stations while Kaelin
continues the song. Rolls determine cost and complications, not whether one
failed check erases all progress.

Round one, the Queen bargains through the mirrors. Round two, two partially
awakened guardians try to interrupt Kaelin or remove the lock-token while hands
rise from cracks in the floor. Round three, the broken chain lashes through the
room. Afterward, someone other than Kaelin must answer her unfinished final
phrase, and Kaelin chooses which way to turn the key.

## Outcome guidance
- closure: the doors slam fully open, the party sees Vaelithra young and furious, the chains pass through them and strike the bier, and the doors close from the inside outward
- partial success: the crypt closes but the awakened mage escapes upward
- costly success: a party member becomes a second bearer of the living lock
- delayed success: the seal holds for one year and one day
- Queen's bargain: Vaelithra closes the crypt for a freely sworn promise
- full failure: Vaelithra remains bound while the web-sleep city awakens and the party must escape
- deliberate opening: only the party knowingly completing the descending cadence can free Vaelithra; this is a campaign-changing choice, not surprise combat
