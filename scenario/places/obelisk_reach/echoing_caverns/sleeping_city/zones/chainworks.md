# Chainworks

## Description
A tall service gallery runs parallel to the avenue. Seven spectral chains pass
through stone apertures toward the crypt while dwarven drums and elven tuning
vanes share the walls. Four are sound, two are frayed, and the seventh hangs
severed. Each contains a future surrendered by an original binder: a prosperous
dwarven city, a continuing elven bloodline, a peace that prevented a war, a
child never born, a marriage never made, a journey never taken, and the Queen's
own future in which she died mortal.

The upper and lower doors make this a bypass around the western side of the
collapsed rotunda. The Queen whispers through the severed seventh chain.
Stabilizing either frayed chain removes a complication from the final ritual;
the party need not repair both.
