# Sleeping District-1

## Description
The door opens onto a city rather than a room. Streets, balconies, workshops,
and homes descend into violet distance, every opening veiled by temporal web.
Families and laborers are visible within. This sight establishes that the
sleepers are an imprisoned population as well as a potential army.

The seal is currently impassable. It offers scale, moral context, and another
route for clues, not a second dungeon to clear.
