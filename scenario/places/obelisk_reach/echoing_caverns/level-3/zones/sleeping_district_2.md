# Sleeping District-2

## Description
West of the avenue, another sleeping neighborhood stretches behind temporal
webbing. Drow families stand motionless in doorways and balconies. The web
blocks entry beyond the threshold; the district is a view into the imprisoned
city rather than an open route.

## Ambient
- light: dim violet pulses through suspended webbing
- sound: silence beyond the seal
- footing: fitted stone at the sealed threshold
