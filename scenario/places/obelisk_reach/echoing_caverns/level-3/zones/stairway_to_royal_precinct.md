# Stairway to Royal Precinct

## Description
The ceremonial avenue narrows into a stone stairway descending toward the
Royal Precinct. Faded royal motifs replace the civic mosaics. Cold air rises
from the chambers below.

## Ambient
- light: dim violet web-light fading toward the lower stairs
- sound: footsteps echo from the precinct below
- footing: worn stone steps
