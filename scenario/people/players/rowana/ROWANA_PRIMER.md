# Playing Rowana — A Quick Guide for Amy

Rowana is a nature healer: part herbalist, part battlefield medic, part person who can turn into an animal when things get weird. Mechanically, she's a support and control character — she's not going to hit the hardest, but she can heal, hinder enemies, and bail out of trouble in ways nobody else at the table can.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 30 ft (about six squares on a battle map).
- **An Action** — the "big thing" you do: cast a spell, attack, or use an ability.
- **A Bonus Action** — a smaller extra thing, but *only* if something you have specifically says "bonus action." Most turns you won't use one.

You can do these in any order, and you can split your movement before and after your action.

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Someone's badly hurt →** cast **Cure Wounds** (touch them, action) to heal them.
- **A cluster of enemies is coming →** cast **Entangle** — it fills a 20-ft square with grabbing vines. No attack roll; *they* roll a save (see below).
- **You want to just hit something →** say "shillelagh" as a bonus action once per fight (it makes your quarterstaff hit harder using your brains instead of your muscle), then attack with the quarterstaff.
- **Nothing urgent →** Faerie Fire makes a group of enemies easier for the whole party to hit. Great opener.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Wild Shape (2 per rest):** As an action, turn into a small animal. Great for scouting, squeezing through gaps, or escaping a bad fight — not for winning fights outright at this level. Ask me (the DM) what's a good shape for the situation if you're not sure.
- **Prepared spells:** Each morning after a long rest, you pick which spells from your list are "loaded." The sheet shows a reasonable default — you don't need to change it unless you want to.
- **Concentration:** Some spells (Entangle, Barkskin, Spike Growth) need your ongoing focus. You can only concentrate on *one* at a time, and getting hit can break it (you'll roll a save to keep it up).

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
Two different ways your spells/attacks work:
- **You attack, they defend:** you roll a d20 + your attack bonus (+5 for spells) against their Armor Class. Higher than their AC = hit.
- **You cast, they resist:** for spells like Entangle or Faerie Fire, *they* roll a d20 + their own modifier against your spell save DC (13). If they roll under 13, it works on them.

Your character sheet tells you which each spell is — I'm happy to just tell you in the moment too.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- Optimizing which animal to Wild Shape into — pick something that sounds fun or useful and we'll figure out the details together.
- The exact math behind Circle of the Land features — I'll flag when they matter.

## Ask Me When
- You're not sure if a spell would help in a specific situation — just describe what you want to happen and I'll tell you which spell (if any) does that.
- You want to try something not written on the sheet — nature magic is flexible, and often the answer is "sure, roll for it."
