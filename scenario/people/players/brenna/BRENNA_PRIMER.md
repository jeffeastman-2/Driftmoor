# Playing Brenna — A Quick Guide for Ann

Brenna is the party's blade: a professional soldier with a greatsword, trained in battlefield tricks that let her control a fight instead of just swinging wildly. No spells to track — Brenna's whole kit is "hit things, cleverly." Good pick if you'd rather learn combat first and magic later.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 30 ft.
- **An Action** — almost always "Attack" for you.
- **A Bonus Action** — only usable if something specifically says "bonus action" (Second Wind does).

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Just fighting normally →** Attack with your greatsword. Simple, reliable, always fine.
- **Worried you'll miss →** add a **Precision Attack** — spend one of your superiority dice to add extra to your attack roll, decided *after* you see you might've missed.
- **Want to knock them down →** **Trip Attack** — on a hit, add a superiority die to the damage and they have to make a Strength save or fall prone (great for setting up allies).
- **They just missed you in melee →** **Riposte** (reaction, free) — get an immediate free attack back, with an extra superiority die of damage.
- **You're hurt and mid-fight →** **Second Wind** (bonus action) — heal 1d10+3 HP right now, once per short rest.
- **You need one huge turn →** **Action Surge** — take a whole extra action (meaning two attacks' worth of actions this turn). Once per short rest; save it for when it counts.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Superiority dice (4 total):** these are what make you a Battle Master instead of a plain fighter. Attach one to an attack for Precision, Trip, or Riposte. They refill completely after any short or long rest, so use them — don't hoard them to the end of the day.
- **Great Weapon Fighting:** when you roll damage with your greatsword and get a 1 or 2 on a damage die, you reroll it. This is automatic — just roll damage as normal and reroll any 1s or 2s you see.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
- **You attack, they defend:** this is basically everything you do — roll a d20 + your attack bonus against their Armor Class.
- **They resist your maneuver:** Trip Attack is the one exception — if you hit, *they* roll a Strength save (DC 13) to avoid falling prone. You don't roll for that part.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- Which maneuver is "optimal" — Precision Attack when you're not sure you'll hit, Trip Attack when you want to help the party, Riposte happens automatically off a miss. You'll get a feel for it fast.
- Superiority dice math — just tell me you're using one and I'll help you add it up.

## Ask Me When
- You want to try shoving someone, grappling, breaking down a door, or some other improvised use of a big person with a big sword — that's well within Brenna's wheelhouse even if it's not written on the sheet.
