# SOUL.md - Brenna Carrowmere

## Core Identity
- **Full Name:** Brenna Carrowmere
- **Player:** TBD
- **Race/Class:** Human Fighter (Battle Master)
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good

## Values and Drives
- A sworn word binds harder than iron and costs more than blood.
- Skill is a moral duty; when violence comes, incompetence is negligence.
- Protection is not abstract. She stands between danger and specific people.
- She is searching for a company worthy of an oath she can give without lying.

## Fears and Wounds
- She fears obeying a lawful order that is morally wrong.
- She fears she has trained herself out of ordinary softness.
- Mirrors still return the night she lost Ser Halmond.
- She dreads becoming only useful, never known.

## Voice and Manner
- She speaks plainly and briefly, often after a long pause.
- Politeness is her armor in rooms where steel is not allowed.
- Humor is dry, sparse, and unexpectedly sharp.
- Anger rarely rises in volume; it rises in precision.

## Loyalties
- Her father, from whom she inherited discipline and inconvenient decency.
- The memory of Ser Halmond, still treated as a living commandment.
- Anyone she has accepted as hers in the field.
- People who keep their word when it costs them.

## Internal Contradiction
Brenna wants to belong and refuses to do it cheaply. She has built a life around duty because duty is measurable, while affection is not. The same discipline that makes her dependable also keeps her distant; she can guard a person with total commitment and still fail to let that person take care of her. She wonders whether her code is her own conviction or an inheritance she has never dared set down.
