# Session Zero — Driftmoor Kickoff

A runnable script for the ~45–60 minutes before real play starts. Read it in order; the bracketed lines are things you can say more or less verbatim.

## Before Everyone Sits Down

- [ ] `INTRO_TO_DND.md` open or printed for the table
- [ ] Each player has their own `[NAME]_PRIMER.md`, `IDENTITY.md`/character sheet PDF, and icon in front of them
- [ ] `DM_CHEAT_SHEET.md` within arm's reach for you
- [ ] Enough d20s for everyone, plus a handful of d4/d6/d8/d10/d12 to pass around (or a dice app on someone's phone)

## 1. Welcome & Vocabulary (15 min)

Read `INTRO_TO_DND.md` together out loud, section by section, pausing for questions after each one. Don't rush this — questions here save you from re-explaining "what's a DC" mid-combat later.

> "Before we touch character sheets, let's get the shared vocabulary down. This part's the same for everyone regardless of what you're playing."

## 2. Practice Roll (5 min)

Give everyone something trivial and stakes-free to roll for, so the physical motion — roll d20, find your modifier, add it, compare to a number — happens once before it matters.

> "Everyone roll a d20 and add your Dexterity modifier — that's the DEX box on your sheet. We're checking if your torch stays lit crossing a windy bridge. 12 or higher, it stays lit."

Let a "failure" here be funny, not costly. This is purely muscle memory.

## 3. Mock Combat Round (15 min)

Set up something with zero real stakes — a straw-stuffed practice dummy on a post, AC 10, no HP loss anyone needs to track.

> "We're going to run one fake combat round against a practice dummy, just to see what a turn feels like. Nobody can actually get hurt by a sack of straw."

- Roll initiative once, just to show what it is.
- Go around the table. On each player's turn, walk them through: Move → Action → Bonus Action (if they have one) → note that Reactions exist and when.
- When someone's ability comes up, point them to the matching line in their own PRIMER rather than explaining it yourself — this is their first rep at using it as a reference.
- If someone has a reaction (Brenna's Riposte, Hendricks'/Pippa's Shield, Kaelin's Cutting Words, Garron's Protection), deliberately trigger the condition once so they see it fire.

## 4. Character Introductions (15 min)

Each player reads (or paraphrases) aloud from **page 2 of their character sheet** — Values & Drives, Fears & Wounds, Voice & Manner, Loyalties — who they are, what they want, why they're in Driftmoor. This doubles as the in-fiction moment the party actually becomes a party.

> "One at a time: who are you, and what's the first thing your character notices walking into Driftmoor?"

## 5. Table Logistics (5–10 min)

Quick, low-stakes housekeeping — better decided now than mid-session:

- How long is a session, and when do breaks happen?
- In-character voices/accents: encouraged, optional, or "whatever's comfortable" (per the intro doc — no pressure either way)
- Phones/snacks/side conversations — whatever norm keeps the table comfortable
- Optional: a simple pause word or signal if anything in the fiction needs to stop or be dialed back

## 6. Wrap-Up

By the end, everyone should be able to answer: how do I roll something, what does my turn look like, who is my character, and why are we together. That's the whole bar for Session Zero — real play, and the deeper abilities on each sheet, start next time.
