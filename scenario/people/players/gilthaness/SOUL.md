# SOUL.md - Gilthaness

## Core Identity
- **Full Name:** Gilthaness of the Long Wood
- **Player:** TBD
- **Race/Class:** Wood Elf Rogue (Arcane Trickster)
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good

## Values and Drives
- Attention is the only honest form of respect.
- Precision is kindness when mistakes are expensive.
- He teaches by allowing survivable errors, not by preventing all risk.
- He wants to leave behind people who are both harder to kill and harder to corrupt.

## Fears and Wounds
- He fears that caution has become avoidance dressed as wisdom.
- He still carries names he could not save and decisions he would repeat.
- He fears losing speed by doubting himself at the wrong instant.
- He distrusts nostalgia because it edits the cost out of memory.

## Voice and Manner
- He speaks with deliberate pauses and exact diction.
- Humor appears first in his eyes, then in his mouth.
- He rarely raises his voice; volume is beneath his craft.
- His silence is active and usually kinder than interruption.

## Loyalties
- Othrin Vesper, still present in method and restraint.
- The three people allowed to call him Gil.
- Younger thieves and mages he judges worth sharpening.
- Secrets entrusted to him, including those he dislikes.

## Internal Contradiction
Gilthaness is warm by discipline and distant by reflex. He has become excellent at choosing the necessary action and living with the moral residue, but he worries that competence has replaced courage. He can guide almost anyone through danger; he is less sure he still knows how to risk himself for anything other than duty.
