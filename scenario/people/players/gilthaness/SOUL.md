# SOUL.md - Gilthaness

## Core Identity
- **Full Name:** Gilthaness of the Long Wood
- **Player:** TBD
- **Race/Class:** Wood Elf Rogue (Arcane Trickster)
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good

## Values and Drives
- Attention is the only honest form of respect.
- Precision is kindness when mistakes are expensive.
- She teaches by allowing survivable errors, not by preventing all risk.
- She wants to leave behind people who are both harder to kill and harder to corrupt.

## Fears and Wounds
- She fears that caution has become avoidance dressed as wisdom.
- She still carries names she could not save and decisions she would repeat.
- She fears losing speed by doubting herself at the wrong instant.
- She distrusts nostalgia because it edits the cost out of memory.

## Voice and Manner
- She speaks with deliberate pauses and exact diction.
- Humor appears first in her eyes, then in her mouth.
- She rarely raises her voice; volume is beneath her craft.
- Her silence is active and usually kinder than interruption.

## Loyalties
- Othrin Vesper, still present in method and restraint.
- The three people allowed to call her Gil.
- Younger thieves and mages she judges worth sharpening.
- Secrets entrusted to her, including those she dislikes.

## Internal Contradiction
Gilthaness is warm by discipline and distant by reflex. She has become excellent at choosing the necessary action and living with the moral residue, but she worries that competence has replaced courage. She can guide almost anyone through danger; she is less sure she still knows how to risk herself for anything other than duty.
