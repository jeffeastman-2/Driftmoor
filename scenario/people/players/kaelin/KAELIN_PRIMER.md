# Playing Kaelin — A Quick Guide for Neala

Kaelin is the party's voice: a performer who talks her way through problems, lifts the whole group up with a word, and can turn a song into a weapon when she has to. Mechanically she's a support-and-face character — great at making other people better, decent at hurting enemies from a distance, not built to stand in the front line.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 30 ft.
- **An Action** — the "big thing" you do: cast a spell, attack, or use an ability.
- **A Bonus Action** — a smaller extra thing, but *only* if something specifically says "bonus action." Bardic Inspiration and Healing Word both use one.

You can split these up however makes sense during your turn.

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **An ally's about to roll something important →** Bardic Inspiration (bonus action, 60 ft) — give them a d6 to add to their next attack, check, or save. You can't use it on yourself.
- **Someone's hurt and it can't wait →** Healing Word (bonus action, 60 ft, no need to touch them) — small heal, but doesn't cost your whole turn, so you can still act after.
- **You want to hurt something →** Vicious Mockery (cantrip, no attack roll needed — they make a save) does a little damage and makes their next attack worse. Dissonant Whispers hits harder and can force them to flee.
- **A fight already went someone's way →** Cutting Words (reaction) — spend a Bardic Inspiration die to subtract from an enemy's attack, check, or damage roll, right after they roll but before you know if it worked.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Jack of All Trades:** you're a little bit good at everything — add a small bonus to any ability check you're not already proficient in. This is automatic; you don't have to remember to use it.
- **Song of Rest:** during a short rest, allies healing up get a little extra back if you're around. Also automatic.
- **Spells known are fixed:** unlike some casters, you don't "prepare" a different list each day — the spells on your sheet are simply what you know and can always cast.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
- **You attack, they defend:** normal weapon attacks (like your rapier) work this way — you roll, compare to their AC.
- **You cast, they resist:** Vicious Mockery and Dissonant Whispers make *them* roll a save (DC 14) against your magic. You don't roll anything for these — just describe the taunt or the verse and let the dice land.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- Micromanaging who gets Bardic Inspiration — giving it to whoever's about to do something exciting is always a reasonable call.
- The exact rules text on Cutting Words — I'll remind you when it's a good moment to use it.

## Ask Me When
- You want to try talking your way out of (or into) something — Persuasion and Performance are your strongest stats by far, use them liberally.
- You're not sure whether a situation calls for Inspiration, a heal, or an attack — describe what you're worried about and I'll help you pick.
