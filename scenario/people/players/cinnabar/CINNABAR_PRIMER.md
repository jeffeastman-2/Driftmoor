# Playing Cinnabar — A Quick Guide for MC

Cinnabar is the party's sneak: quick, quiet, and always looking for the angle nobody else noticed. She hits hard when she catches someone off guard, moves like it costs her nothing, and has a few small magic tricks for getting into places she shouldn't be.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 30 ft.
- **An Action** — attack, cast a spell, or use an ability.
- **A Bonus Action** — Cunning Action (Dash, Disengage, or Hide) uses this. You'll use your bonus action almost every turn — it's one of your best tools.

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Want your Sneak Attack to land →** you need either advantage on the attack, or an ally within 5 ft of your target. Hiding first (bonus action) is the easiest way to get advantage on your next hit.
- **Getting ganged up on →** **Disengage** (bonus action) then move away — nobody gets a free hit on you as you leave.
- **Need to close distance or flee fast →** **Dash** (bonus action) doubles your movement this turn.
- **Something dangerous needs to be shut down →** **Tasha's Hideous Laughter** — if it lands, that creature falls down laughing and can't act. Great opener on the scariest thing in the room.
- **Need a distraction →** **Silent Image** creates a moving, silent illusion — useful for luring guards away or faking a threat.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Sneak Attack (2d6, once per turn):** only works with advantage, or when an ally is adjacent to your target — check for one of those before counting on it.
- **Cunning Action:** free extra mobility every turn via your bonus action. This is what makes you hard to pin down.
- **Mage Hand Legerdemain:** your Mage Hand cantrip can be invisible and pick locks, disarm traps, or plant/lift objects at range — this is Cinnabar's signature "I do the impossible heist thing" tool. Use it liberally and creatively.
- **Expertise:** your Stealth and Thieves' Tools bonuses are doubled — you're exceptional at both, lean on them.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
- **You attack, they defend:** rapier and shortbow work this way — roll d20 + attack bonus against their AC.
- **You cast, they resist:** Tasha's Hideous Laughter makes *them* roll a Wisdom save (DC 12). You don't roll for it — describe the joke or the moment and let their dice decide.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- You only have 2 first-level spell slots — save Tasha's Hideous Laughter for something that's actually dangerous, not routine fights.
- Nobody expects perfect stealth tactics on turn one; ask and we'll figure out positioning together.

## Ask Me When
- You want to try something sneaky that's not written on the sheet — pickpocketing, forging a signature, faking your way past a guard. This is exactly Cinnabar's specialty, so ask often.
