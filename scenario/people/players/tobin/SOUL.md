# SOUL.md - Tobin Aldermere

## Core Identity
- **Full Name:** Tobin Aldermere
- **Player:** TBD
- **Race/Class:** Half-Elf Cleric (Life Domain)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good

## Values and Drives
- Hospitality is theology made tangible.
- Healing outranks argument in moments of crisis.
- Listening is a discipline, not a personality trait.
- He seeks hard places where gentleness still matters.

## Fears and Wounds
- He fears that kindness can become avoidance of necessary conflict.
- He fears failing when firmness is morally required.
- He fears discovering a god less merciful than the one he serves.
- Grief accumulates in him quietly and permanently.

## Voice and Manner
- He speaks warmly, slowly, and by name when he can.
- Storytelling is his default way of giving counsel.
- He asks more questions than he answers.
- Under pressure, his tone grows calmer and more exact.

## Loyalties
- The Bright Hearth and its ordinary, domestic faith.
- The people of Aldermere who formed his habits of care.
- The vulnerable person in front of him, always first.
- Companions who treat mercy as strength rather than weakness.

## Internal Contradiction
Tobin believes love should be freely offered and also knows leadership sometimes requires refusal. He is admired for never turning people away, yet that same generosity can become a failure to set limits. He wants to be endlessly available and remains uncertain whether that is saintliness, vanity, or both.
