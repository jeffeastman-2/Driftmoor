# Playing Tobin — A Quick Guide for Dave

Tobin is the party's healer and anchor: warm, steady, and hard to bring down thanks to good armor and a shield. His whole job is keeping everyone else standing — and he's very good at it.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 30 ft.
- **An Action** — cast a spell or attack.
- **A Bonus Action** — several of your best spells use this (Healing Word, Spiritual Weapon, Sanctuary, Shield of Faith), so you'll often use your action *and* bonus action for two different things in one turn.

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Someone's badly hurt mid-fight →** **Healing Word** (bonus action, 60 ft, no need to touch them) — then you still have your action free for something else that turn.
- **Someone's dying (at 0 HP) →** **Spare the Dying** (cantrip, touch) — stabilizes them immediately, no roll needed.
- **A big heal is worth the whole turn →** **Cure Wounds** (action, touch) — bigger than Healing Word, best used when you're not also trying to do something else that turn.
- **You want to hurt something instead of heal →** **Sacred Flame** cantrip (unlimited use) or **Guiding Bolt** (bigger hit, and their next attacker gets advantage against them).
- **Before a fight starts →** **Bless** (up to 3 allies get better attacks and saves) or **Shield of Faith** (+2 AC on one ally).

## Your Special Tricks
- **Disciple of Life:** your healing spells restore extra HP automatically — the numbers on your sheet already include the bonus, so just use them as written.
- **Channel Divinity (once per rest):** either **Turn Undead** (nearby undead must save or flee) or **Preserve Life** (instantly hand out 15 HP total across your hurt allies, split however you like). Save this for when a fight is going badly — it's a strong once-a-rest option.
- **Good armor:** chain mail and shield put your AC at 18 — you can afford to stand near the fight without much risk, which is exactly where a healer needs to be.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
- **You attack, they defend:** Guiding Bolt is a normal spell attack roll (+5) against their AC.
- **You cast, they resist:** Sacred Flame makes *them* roll a Dexterity save (DC 13) — you don't roll anything, just describe the flame and let their dice decide.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- Picking the "right" prepared spell — the list on your sheet covers the situations that come up most; ask me in the moment if you want something different.
- Whether to heal or attack — as a rule of thumb, heal when someone's genuinely at risk, otherwise Sacred Flame is always a fine default.

## Ask Me When
- You want to use Channel Divinity — tell me the situation and I'll help you decide between Turn Undead and Preserve Life.
- You want to counsel, comfort, or talk someone down — that's very much in character for Tobin, and often as useful as a spell.
