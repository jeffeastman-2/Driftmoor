## Physical Description
Broad and grey-bearded even by dwarven standards, Garron stands like a doorway nothing gets through without his say-so. His beard is bound in three iron rings, one etched with a psalm he recited over his brother's grave. He wears dented chain mail over a threadbare devotional tabard, and his shield bears a hand-hammered sunburst that has taken more blows than his face has.
