# Playing Garron — A Quick Guide for Tom

Garron is a holy knight: heavily armored, hard to hurt, and built to stand in front of the people who can't take a hit themselves. Mechanically he's a front-line tank with a bit of healing and a big burst-damage trick when he needs it (Divine Smite).

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 25 ft (dwarves are a little slower than most).
- **An Action** — the "big thing" you do: attack, cast a spell, or use an ability.
- **A Bonus Action** — a smaller extra thing, but *only* if something you have specifically says "bonus action." Most turns you won't use one.

You can split these up however makes sense — move partway, act, move the rest.

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Just fighting normally →** attack with your longsword (action). Simple, reliable, always fine.
- **You land a solid hit and want to make it count →** Divine Smite — right after you hit, you can burn a spell slot to add a big chunk of extra damage. Best saved for a hit that already looks good, or against something that seems important/nasty. You don't have to decide until after you know you've hit.
- **An ally next to you is about to get hit →** your shield lets you impose disadvantage on that attack as a reaction (free, no cost) — you don't even need to think about this ahead of time, just tell me you're using it when it comes up.
- **Someone's hurt and you're not in the middle of a crisis →** Lay on Hands — touch them, no action even needed to spend from the pool for pure healing, restore HP straight from your 15-point pool. Great for topping people off between fights.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Lay on Hands (15 HP per rest):** A pool of healing you spend by touch, in any amount. Also cures poison/disease if you spend 5 points on it. Doesn't require a spell slot — just touch and go.
- **Divine Sense (3 per rest):** As an action, sense if anything undead, fiendish, or celestial is nearby. Good for "does this feel wrong" moments — ask me and I'll tell you what you sense.
- **Channel Divinity (1 per rest):** A once-per-rest power move. Either make one weapon supernaturally accurate and glowing for a minute (Sacred Weapon), or try to force nearby undead/fiends to flee (Turn the Unholy). Save it for when a fight is already going badly, or when the story moment calls for it.
- **Prepared spells:** Each morning after a long rest, you pick which spells are "loaded" from your list. The sheet shows a reasonable default — no need to change it unless you want to.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
Two different ways your abilities work:
- **You attack, they defend:** roll a d20 + your attack bonus against their Armor Class. Higher than their AC = hit. This is what you'll do most turns.
- **You cast, they resist:** for spells like Bless or Sanctuary, *they* (or your allies, for Bless) roll against a DC instead of you rolling to hit.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- The exact damage math on Divine Smite — I'll help you figure out the dice in the moment.
- Optimizing which spell slots to save vs. spend — as a rule of thumb, using one on a smite mid-fight is almost always fine.

## Ask Me When
- You're deciding whether to smite — tell me you hit, and I'll help you weigh "save it" vs. "use it now."
- You want to try protecting someone or intimidating someone in a way that's not written on the sheet — a devoted knight has more options than his stat block; just describe what you want to do.
