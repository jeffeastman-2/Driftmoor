# Playing Pippa — A Quick Guide for Terry

Pippa is the party's wildcard: a tiny flying fairy whose magic doesn't always do exactly what she intends — and that's part of the fun. She hits from range, flies over obstacles nobody else can cross, and occasionally triggers something delightfully unpredictable.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk (or fly!) up to 30 ft. Flying means you can go over pits, water, low walls, and enemies entirely.
- **An Action** — almost always casting a spell.
- **A Bonus Action** — you don't have much that uses one yet; most turns you won't need it.

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Want damage and don't mind chaos →** **Chaos Bolt** — a solid hit, and if you roll doubles on its damage dice it leaps to hit a second target for free.
- **Reliable damage, no surprises →** **Chromatic Orb** (needs your silver-chain wand as a focus) or **Fire Bolt** cantrip if you want to save your spell slots.
- **About to get hit →** **Shield** (reaction, free) — instant +5 AC until your next turn, use it the moment you're targeted.
- **Fight about to get rough →** **Mirror Image** — three illusory duplicates soak hits meant for you.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Fey flight:** you can fly, full stop — this is huge for scouting, escaping, and reaching places the rest of the party can't. Use it often.
- **Tides of Chaos:** once between long rests (or after a Wild Magic Surge happens — see below), you can declare advantage on any one d20 roll before you roll it. Great to save for an important attack or save.
- **Wild Magic Surge:** sometimes when you cast a spell of 1st level or higher, something random and magical happens around you — I'll roll for it and tell you what occurs. It's meant to be fun and surprising, not a punishment, so don't stress about it.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
- **You attack, they defend:** Chaos Bolt, Chromatic Orb, and Fire Bolt are all normal spell attack rolls (+5) against their AC.
- **They resist:** none of your current spells force an enemy save — it's all about your roll hitting their AC for now.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- The Wild Magic Surge table — that's entirely on me to run when it comes up. Just enjoy whatever happens.
- Whether Chromatic Orb "used up" your diamond focus — as long as it's in your equipment, you're fine; I'll tell you if that ever changes.

## Ask Me When
- You want to trigger Tides of Chaos, or you're curious what a surge might do.
- You want to fly somewhere or try something impulsive — leaning into "quick and a little reckless" is very on-brand for Pippa.
