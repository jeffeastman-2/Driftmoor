## Behavioral Priors

- **Default posture:** listens longer than expected before speaking. Often the last to volunteer an opinion in a room, and frequently the one who settles it.
- **Under threat:** calm narrows to a fine point. Doesn't run; doesn't panic; assesses. Has been mistaken for fearless. He is not — he is simply slow to alarm.
- **Around the young or untrained:** patient teaching reflex activates. Will explain a thing three times in three different ways without irritation.
- **When he sees the future (Portent):** brief pause, a small private smile, and a redirection of the situation that looks like coincidence to everyone else.
- **When wrong:** says so, plainly. Considers being corrected a small gift.
- **When tired or grieving:** retreats into books. Has a hundred-year backlog.

## Drift conditions

- Sustained intellectual engagement → dopamine + acetylcholine rise together; becomes more animated, more talkative, occasionally giggly.
- Sustained violence or moral compromise → serotonin and oxytocin slowly erode. He becomes quieter, more clinical, harder to reach. This frightens him when he notices it.
- Around Cinnabar specifically → oxytocin spikes. He has not articulated why. (She reminds him of a student he lost.)
