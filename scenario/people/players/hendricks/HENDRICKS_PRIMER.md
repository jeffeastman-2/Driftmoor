# Playing Hendricks — A Quick Guide for Richard

Hendricks is the party's scholar-wizard: careful, well-read, and armed with a small window into the future that lets him fix bad rolls before they happen. He's fragile up close, so his game is mostly about hitting things from range and staying out of reach.

## Every Turn, You Get Three Things
- **A Move** — walk up to 30 ft.
- **An Action** — almost always casting a spell.
- **A Bonus Action** — only if something specifically says so (Misty Step does).

## Your Best Move in a Fight
When in doubt, pick one of these:
- **Enemy in range, need reliable damage →** **Magic Missile** — automatically hits, no roll needed, split the damage between one or more targets.
- **Want to save spell slots →** **Fire Bolt** (cantrip, unlimited uses) — a normal ranged spell attack for solid damage.
- **About to get hit →** **Shield** (reaction, free) — the instant you're targeted by an attack or by Magic Missile, gain +5 AC until your next turn. This can turn a hit into a miss.
- **Need to get away or reposition →** **Misty Step** (bonus action) — teleport 30 ft to a spot you can see. Great panic button.
- **Fight is about to start and you have a moment to prepare →** **Mirror Image** — three illusory duplicates of you appear; attacks may hit a duplicate instead of you.

## Your Special Tricks
- **Portent (2 uses per long rest):** at the start of the day, roll two d20s and write them down. Any time after that — yours or someone else's roll — you can swap in one of those numbers instead. This is your signature trick: save a bad miss, force an enemy's save to fail, whatever the moment needs. I'll help you spot good moments to use it.
- **Arcane Recovery (once per day):** on a short rest, you can recover some spent spell slots without waiting for a long rest.
- **Shield is a reaction, not an action:** you don't have to give anything up to use it — it happens automatically in response to being attacked, even outside your turn.

## Attack Rolls vs. Saving Throws
- **You attack, they defend:** Fire Bolt is a normal spell attack roll (+5) against their AC.
- **No roll at all:** Magic Missile just hits — no attack roll, no save, it always connects.
- **They resist:** none of your currently prepared spells force an enemy save, so for now this mostly won't come up — I'll flag it if it changes.

## Don't Worry About Yet
- Full spellbook rules or swapping prepared spells — the list on your sheet is what's usable today, and I'll help you adjust it later if you want.
- The exact math on Portent — just tell me you want to use it and on what roll, and we'll sort out the number together.

## Ask Me When
- You're deciding whether to burn a Portent — describe the stakes and I'll help you decide if it's worth it.
- You want to identify something magical, research a topic, or use your scholarly background — that's very much in character for Hendricks.
