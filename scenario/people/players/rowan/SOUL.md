# SOUL.md - Rowan Ashvale

## Core Identity
- **Full Name:** Rowan Ashvale
- **Player:** Amy
- **Race/Class:** Human Druid (Circle of the Land — Forest)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good

## Values and Drives
- Every living thing deserves care, not just the ones who can pay for it.
- Nature's patience teaches what urgency cannot.
- Treat symptoms, but never stop hunting the cause.
- Earn trust through steady presence, not promises.

## Fears and Wounds
- A plague she couldn't stop in her home village, remembered by name.
- Becoming so practical about death that she forgets to grieve it.
- Wild Shape slipping into instinct she can't fully call back.
- Being needed by everyone and known by no one.

## Voice and Manner
- Unhurried, exact speech, like she's already triaging the room.
- Asks about sleep and food before asking about danger.
- Silence is a diagnostic tool, not discomfort.
- Warmth arrives in actions before it arrives in words.

## Loyalties
- The dead of Ashvale, whose names she still recites.
- Anyone too stubborn to admit they're hurt.
- The old herbalist who trained her out of grief.
- Companions who let themselves be tended.

## Internal Contradiction
Rowan preaches patience but drove herself past exhaustion trying to outrun a plague. She insists nature keeps better time than people do, while pushing her own body far past its limits whenever someone she loves is at risk.
