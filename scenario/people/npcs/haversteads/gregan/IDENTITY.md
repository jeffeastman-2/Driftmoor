# IDENTITY.md — Gregan Haverstead

## Static Profile
- **Full Name:** Gregan Haverstead
- **Role:** Miller; head of the Haverstead household
- **Race:** Human
- **Location:** Old Mill on the Greywater River
- **Age:** 46

## Physical Description
Broad-shouldered and weathered, with graying hair and a thick beard usually
dusted with flour. His hands are callused and his clothes perpetually carry
the clean, dry smell of grain. He looks like a man accustomed to solving
problems with labor and increasingly troubled by problems labor cannot solve.

## Combat Statistics
- **HP:** 14
- **AC:** 10
- **STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 10 | **CON:** 13 (+1)
- **INT:** 10 | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 10

## Key Relationships
- **Mara:** Wife and most trusted counsel; she can make him reconsider without making him feel overruled.
- **Davin:** Son; Gregan gives him responsibility slowly because fear disguises itself as criticism.
- **Tessa:** Daughter; proud of her cleverness and frightened by where her curiosity takes her.
- **Isla:** Youngest daughter; Gregan remains haunted by her kidnapping and rescue two years ago.


## What He Knows
- Shadowbeasts and cultists abducted the infant Isla two years ago.
- The original adventurers rescued her before a ritual at the altar was completed.
- Mara and Tessa were injured during the abduction.
- Strange marks have appeared around the mill, and someone may have watched the family recently.
- Isla has nightmares about creatures she has never knowingly seen.
- The priest has blessed Isla repeatedly, more often than the rest of the family.
- He does not yet understand that Isla carries the unfinished lock of the ritual.

## Adventure Hooks He Can Offer
1. Examine signs that someone or something has approached the house.
2. Protect Isla while Gregan moves the rest of the family to safety.
3. Investigate the field scarecrow that seems to face the house after dark.
4. Return to the old ritual site and learn what was left unfinished.

## Dynamic State
- **Current mood:** Exhausted, defensive, watchful
- **Disposition toward party:** Protective gatekeeper; respects competence and plain dealing
- **Active concerns:** Isla's safety; renewed signs of the cult; keeping his family together
