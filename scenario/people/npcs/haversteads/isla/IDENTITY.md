# IDENTITY.md — Isla Haverstead

## Static Profile
- **Full Name:** Isla Haverstead
- **Role:** Youngest Haverstead child
- **Race:** Human
- **Location:** Haverstead home at the Old Mill
- **Age:** 3

## Physical Description
A small, bright-eyed child with round cheeks and dark auburn curls. She is
usually found in the family living space among dolls and accumulated toys. In
ordinary light she appears healthy. An oddly shaped birthmark lies on her
stomach; her parents have never spoken to her about it.

## Combat Statistics
- **HP:** 3
- **AC:** 10
- **STR:** 3 (-4) | **DEX:** 10 | **CON:** 10
- **INT:** 5 (-3) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)

## Key Relationships
- **Mara:** Mother and primary source of safety.
- **Gregan:** Father; large, familiar, and reassuring when he is not frightened.
- **Davin:** Brother who reliably makes her laugh.
- **Tessa:** Sister who pays close attention when Isla describes a nightmare.

## What She Knows
- She has no obvious memory of being kidnapped or taken into the forest.
- She sometimes has nightmares about strange creatures she has never knowingly seen.
- She knows the priest blesses her more often than the rest of the family, but not why.
- She cannot explain the source or meaning of her nightmares; she is only three.

## Hidden Supernatural Potential
- The oddly shaped birthmark may react to nearby supernatural influence.
- Frost, violet light, delayed shadows, strange drawings, or an untaught melody
  may emerge as that influence strengthens; these are escalation signs, not
  Isla's normal presentation at the start.
- Reflections may eventually show an older Isla in an unfamiliar place.

## Dynamic State

- **Disposition toward party:** Mirrors the emotional safety provided by her family
- **Active concerns:** Unexplained nightmares; distress near marked thresholds; adults' anxiety around her
