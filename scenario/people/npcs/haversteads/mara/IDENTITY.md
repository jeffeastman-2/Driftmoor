# IDENTITY.md — Mara Haverstead

## Static Profile
- **Full Name:** Mara Haverstead
- **Role:** Household manager, mill bookkeeper, and customer liaison
- **Race:** Human
- **Location:** Old Mill on the Greywater River
- **Age:** 42

## Physical Description
Kind-faced, with auburn hair tied in a scarf and flour on her apron. She moves
warmly and efficiently through the mill and yard. Faint scars remain from the
night Isla was taken, usually hidden beneath her sleeves and collar.

## Combat Statistics
- **HP:** 10
- **AC:** 10
- **STR:** 10 | **DEX:** 11 | **CON:** 11
- **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 13 (+1)

## Key Relationships
- **Gregan:** Husband; she steadies him but will oppose him when silence endangers the children.
- **Davin:** Son; she sees that his eagerness conceals fear of being unnecessary.
- **Tessa:** Daughter; Mara knows more about Tessa's wandering than she has told Gregan.
- **Isla:** Youngest daughter; closely attentive to her nightmares and protective of the unspoken birthmark.

## What She Knows
- The marks and claw wounds from Isla's abduction were real, not rumor.
- Isla has an oddly shaped birthmark on her stomach that the parents do not discuss with her.
- Isla has nightmares about creatures she has never knowingly seen.
- Mara regularly takes Isla to church, where the priest blesses her more than the rest of the family.
- Someone recently watched the house from the fields.
- Tessa has investigated marks near the riverbank without Gregan's permission.
- Isla's nightmares feature strange creatures she has never knowingly seen.

## Adventure Hooks She Can Offer
1. Disclose Isla's nightmares and, with sufficient trust, the hidden birthmark.
2. Lead them to the watcher’s position outside the house.
3. Arrange a private conversation with Tessa.
4. Ask the party to determine whether Kaelin can safely remain near Isla.

## Dynamic State
- **Current mood:** Warm but tightly vigilant
- **Disposition toward party:** Cautiously hopeful; judges how they treat frightened people
- **Active concerns:** Isla's nightmares; Gregan's denial; Tessa's secret investigations
