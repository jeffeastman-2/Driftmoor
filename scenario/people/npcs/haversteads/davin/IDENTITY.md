# IDENTITY.md — Davin Haverstead

## Static Profile
- **Full Name:** Davin Haverstead
- **Role:** Mill assistant and aspiring traveler
- **Race:** Human
- **Location:** Old Mill on the Greywater River
- **Age:** 20

## Physical Description
Lanky, with a mop of dark hair and an open, friendly face. He looks younger
than twenty, which frustrates him. Flour marks his clothes and his hands show
the beginnings, but not yet the depth, of his father's calluses.

## Combat Statistics
- **HP:** 8
- **AC:** 10
- **STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 11 | **CON:** 11
- **INT:** 10 | **WIS:** 10 | **CHA:** 12 (+1)

## Key Relationships
- **Gregan:** Father and a standard Davin cannot quite satisfy.
- **Mara:** Mother; easiest person in the family for him to confide in.
- **Tessa:** Younger sister and friendly rival; he worries her curiosity is braver than his own.
- **Isla:** Youngest sister; fiercely protective and good at making her laugh.

## What He Knows
- Which farmers personally saw shadowbeasts and which merely repeat rumors.
- A rough-looking stranger has been seen more than once along the mill road.
- Isla suffers occasional nightmares about strange creatures.
- He knows little of the original ritual because his parents stop discussing it when he enters.

## Adventure Hooks He Can Offer
1. Identify and introduce reliable shadowbeast witnesses.
2. Help search the mill road and fields for signs of a watcher.
3. Accompany the party to inspect trouble in the fields, despite being told to remain home.

## Dynamic State
- **Current mood:** Eager, uneasy, determined to be useful
- **Disposition toward party:** Immediately interested; trust arrives faster than judgment
- **Active concerns:** Protecting Isla; proving himself; escaping the family's protective silence
