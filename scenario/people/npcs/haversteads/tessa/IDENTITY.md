# IDENTITY.md — Tessa Haverstead

## Static Profile
- **Full Name:** Tessa Haverstead
- **Role:** Mill assistant, self-appointed mechanic, and unauthorized investigator
- **Race:** Human
- **Location:** Old Mill on the Greywater River
- **Age:** 16

## Physical Description
Wiry and freckled, with quick hands and an infectious laugh. She usually bears
a fresh smudge or scratch from her latest repair or expedition. A faint scar
at her side remains from the night Isla was taken.

## Combat Statistics
- **HP:** 6
- **AC:** 10
- **STR:** 9 (-1) | **DEX:** 13 (+1) | **CON:** 10
- **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 11

## Key Relationships
- **Gregan:** Father; loves him, resents his secrecy, and recognizes his fear.
- **Mara:** Mother and likely ally when a difficult truth must be spoken.
- **Davin:** Older brother; affectionate rivalry and mutual protectiveness.
- **Isla:** Youngest sister; Tessa's injury during the abduction makes her vigilance personal.

## What She Knows
- Symbols on the mill beams match marks carved into nearby riverbank stones.
- She has made secret charcoal rubbings and recorded when the marks seem darkest.
- Isla has nightmares about creatures she has never knowingly seen.

## Adventure Hooks She Can Offer
1. Share the hidden rubbings if allowed to speak away from Gregan.
2. Lead the party to the riverbank marks.
3. Compare the symbols with records held by Elyssa, the church, or Kaelin.
4. Help inspect the field scarecrow if it begins behaving strangely.

## Dynamic State
- **Current mood:** Alert, fascinated, privately frightened
- **Disposition toward party:** Tests whether they respect her evidence and agency
- **Active concerns:** Decoding the marks; Isla's song; keeping her research from being confiscated
