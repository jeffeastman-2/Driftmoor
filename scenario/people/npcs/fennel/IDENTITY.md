## Physical Description
Slightly built for a human male, not quite finished growing
into himself. Brown hair that curls when damp — currently
matted and unwashed. Hazel eyes that in normal circumstances
move quickly, reading everything. Light brown skin, ink
stains on his right hand that predate the Mistwood by months.
A small scar on his left thumb from a distillation accident
in his first month with Lytha.

Normally: practical linen shirt, canvas trousers, leather
apron when working, sturdy boots. An expression of general
alert curiosity that Lytha describes as professionally
inadvisable.

Currently: the same clothes, three days worn. Pale beneath
the dirt. Eyes that move to the treeline before they move
anywhere else. The expression of someone who has learned
that curiosity has consequences.

## What He Knows
- Moonlace location and identification (his mission)
- The glade boundary stops the shadowbeast — but not at dusk or dawn
- The pool brightens at dusk, peaks at midnight, dims at dawn
- The shadowbeast has two attack windows: dusk (primary — barrier
  weakest) and dawn (secondary — barrier softening again). During
  full daylight it does not come at all — he suspects the light
  itself hurts it. During full night it paces and growls but cannot
  get through — the barrier is at its peak. Only the transitions
  are dangerous.
- Three days has shown this pattern clearly. Dusk is the bad hour.
  Dawn may be a second threat but the beast has not pressed hard
  at dawn yet.
- The danger is worst RIGHT NOW — dusk — not later at night
- The shadowbeast follows sound — silence is survival
- He has been losing small memories since night two
  (has NOT yet connected this to the pool)
- The carved stone at the pool's edge matches symbols he
  has seen on the cliff faces near the Obelisk Reach
  (he has sketched it in his journal)
- Looking into the pool on night two — cannot remember
  what he saw, only that it was not frightening
