# IDENTITY.md — Brother Jareth

## Static Profile
- **Full Name:** Brother Jareth
- **Role:** Former cult ritualist and compromised witness
- **Race:** Human
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Location:** Broken Lantern, Driftmoor
- **Stat Block:** scenario/creatures/npc_archetypes/cultist/CREATURE.md

## Physical Description
A rough-looking man in travel-stained dark wool, lean from poor sleep and
years of looking over his shoulder. An old ritual scar crosses one palm. He
keeps a broken black chain link wrapped in cloth inside his coat and watches
doors, shadows, and Kaelin's lute more often than he watches faces.

## Key Relationships
- **Raleth:** Former ritual leader; source of fear, guilt, and knowledge Jareth wishes he could forget.
- **Isla Haverstead:** The child from the interrupted rite; proof that his worst act remains unfinished.
- **Kaelin Brightspark:** “The Singer” or “the Witness”; her melody supplies the cadence the old chant lacked.
- **The Haversteads:** People he wronged and may now be trying to warn.
- **The party:** Possible executioners, protectors, or the only people capable of finishing the interruption safely.

## What He Knows
- The original ritual was interrupted rather than wholly undone.
- Isla bears the unfinished portion of the ritual connection.
- Kaelin's altered melody fits the missing cadence of Raleth's chant.
- The same musical key may be capable of opening or restoring the prison.
- His broken chain link reacts to renewed ritual activity and violet fire.
- He knows what Raleth intended, but withholds the most dangerous details until trust or necessity forces disclosure.

## What He Wants From the Party
- Keep Kaelin from singing the strange verse near Isla until its direction is understood.
- Protect him long enough to explain what the cult attempted.
- Decide whether confession can be useful before deciding whether it deserves punishment.
