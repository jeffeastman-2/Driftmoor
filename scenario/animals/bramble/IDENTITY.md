## Physical Description
A broad, well-kept bay mare with a dark mane, warm brown coat, and a narrow
white blaze. Her posture is relaxed around Kara and quietly authoritative
among the other horses.

## Behavioral Markers
- Settles when Kara speaks or brushes her.
- Uses stillness and proximity to calm other horses.
- Investigates familiar people by scent before accepting touch.
- Places herself between a panicked herd mate and unfamiliar movement.
