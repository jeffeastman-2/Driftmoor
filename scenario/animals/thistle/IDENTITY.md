## Physical Description
A quick, dapple-gray mare with a dark mane and alert amber-brown eyes. Her
ears rarely stop moving. She carries herself lightly, ready to sidestep before
an uncertain sound becomes a visible threat.

## Behavioral Markers
- Detects unfamiliar scents and distant movement before Bramble does.
- Watches the Mistwood-side entrance and the missing horse's stall.
- Accepts Kara and Bramble as anchors but remains cautious with strangers.
- Warns through fixed attention, breath, and lateral movement before panic.
