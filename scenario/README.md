Driftmoor is a small town perched on a rocky coast where the Greywater River flows into the Azure Sea. Driftmoor is nestled between jagged cliffs and windswept plains, giving it an isolated but picturesque setting. It has a population of around 500: mostly humans, halflings and a few dwarves and elves. A small harbor is sheltered by rocky outcroppings. Wooden piers and fishing boats dominate the scene, with ramshackle homes belonging to the fisherfolk. The waters are teeming with fish, but the occasional shadowy form beneath the surface unnerves the locals. The heart of Driftmoor has modest stone and timber buildings that house several shops: an apothecary, a pawn shop, a rooming house, a tavern, a small church, a barber shop, a stable, a mercantile and the mayor’s hall. A mile upriver are scattered farms growing hardy grains, root vegetables, and some grazing livestock. Farmers often transport their goods downstream to the harbor by raft. It is late fall; the days grow shorter and the harvest is in.

You’ve arrived in the town by various means and at different times and have not yet met. You may now explore the town individually, interact with townsfolk whom you may meet and, if circumstances align, meet some of your fellow adventurers. Please text me privately as you are not yet a group.

Here’s a cohesive backstory that explains how this diverse group of adventurers came together. It weaves their personal goals and histories into a believable reason for their partnership at the start of The Shattered Obelisk.

Backstory: The Bonds of Circumstance

Each member of the party has their own reason for traveling through the frontier region of Driftmoor. Though they began as strangers, their paths converged due to a common threat—and a common opportunity.

Kaelin Brightspark (Halfling Bard)

Kaelin was performing at a roadside tavern near Driftmoor, entertaining a crowd of travelers and locals with a lively tune about a “haunted obelisk.” This song was no coincidence; Kaelin had heard rumors about the strange disturbances in Driftmoor and saw an opportunity to weave a tale worth performing. They were secretly angling for patrons who might pay for their unique skills—be it with music, diplomacy, or clever deception.

Thorgrim Stoneheart (Dwarf Fighter)

Thorgrim was traveling through the region as a self-imposed exile from his homeland, taking odd mercenary jobs to stay afloat. While staying at the same tavern as Kaelin, he overheard the halfling’s song about the obelisk and the troubles in Driftmoor. Recognizing the dangers such disturbances might pose to innocent people, Thorgrim decided to investigate. He struck up a conversation with Kaelin, who gladly welcomed his strength.

Seraphina Duskwhisper (Half-Elf Rogue)

Seraphina was already in Driftmoor, lying low after a botched heist in a nearby town. She had been working with a group of local thieves, but her arcane slip-up during the job drew too much attention. The thieves’ guild marked her as expendable, forcing her into hiding. Hearing rumors of adventurers gathering at the tavern, Seraphina decided to “join up” as a way to escape her predicament and find some fast coin.

Varrak Ironflame (Dragonborn Sorcerer)

Varrak had been wandering for months, seeking knowledge and relics to enhance their understanding of their draconic lineage. The shattered obelisk intrigued them; its fragments were rumored to contain immense magical energy. Varrak heard about Driftmoor through a traveling merchant (Kelem Dustcloak, who may play a role later) and arrived at the tavern just in time to overhear Kaelin’s performance. Though they initially dismissed the bard’s dramatics, Varrak found themselves drawn to the obelisk’s mystery and decided to join this “ragtag band” to secure the artifact for themselves.

Aeliana Moonglade (Wood Elf Ranger)

Aeliana was tracking the shadowbeasts that had begun emerging in the forests near Driftmoor. She saw their presence as a sign that something was deeply wrong with the natural order. Her tracking brought her to the tavern, where she encountered the other adventurers. Though wary at first, Aeliana realized their goals aligned—especially when Thorgrim and Kaelin invited her to join the investigation.

Sister Amara Solbright (Human Cleric)

Amara was sent to Driftmoor by her church after news of the disturbances reached her order. Viewing the obelisk as a possible source of evil, she set out to investigate and assist the locals. She arrived at the tavern where Kaelin was performing and, upon seeing the group forming, offered her services. Amara’s sincerity and calm demeanor quickly won the trust of Thorgrim and Aeliana, and her healing magic made her an invaluable addition to the team.

Father Lilar Hearthsong (Human Cleric)

Lilar was raised in a humble farming village that fell on hard times when disease and famine ravaged his community. When no one else could help, he prayed to the gods of light and was answered with a gift of divine magic. Devoting his life to healing and hope, he joined a traveling clergy, bringing aid to those in need.
