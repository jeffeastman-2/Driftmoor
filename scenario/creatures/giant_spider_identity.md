Giant Spider. CR 1. HP 26. AC 14 (natural armor). Speed 30ft, climb 30ft. STR 14 DEX 16 CON 12 INT 2 WIS 11 CHA 4. Spider Climb: can climb walls and ceilings freely. Web Sense: knows exact location of any creature touching same web. Web Walker: ignores movement restriction from webbing. Attack: one bite per turn. Bite: +5, 1d8+3 piercing, plus DC 11 CON save or take 2d8 poison (half on save); if poison reduces target to 0HP, paralyzed for 1 hour. Web (Recharge 5-6): ranged +5 to hit, 30/60ft, target restrained. Restrained creature can make DC 12 STR check (action) to burst free. Webbing AC 10, HP 5, vulnerable to fire. Ambush predator: drops from ceiling on first creature to enter. Retreats to upper webs if outmatched.
