Scarecrow

A straw field guardian animated by a malign spirit. This one watches the road
to the Haversteads from a field of hardy grain. A broken black chain link is
sewn behind its burlap heart, and its painted face seems to turn toward Isla's
home whenever nobody is looking.

Race: Medium Construct
Alignment: Chaotic Evil
Armor Class: 11
Hit Points: 36 (8d8)
Speed: 30 ft
Challenge: 1 (200 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 11 (+0)
• Dexterity: 13 (+1)
• Constitution: 11 (+0)
• Intelligence: 10 (+0)
• Wisdom: 10 (+0)
• Charisma: 13 (+1)

Damage Vulnerabilities:
• Fire

Damage Resistances:
• Bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

Damage Immunities:
• Poison

Condition Immunities:
• Charmed
• Exhausted
• Frightened
• Paralyzed
• Poisoned
• Unconscious

Senses:
• Darkvision 60 ft.
• Passive Perception 10

Features
• False Appearance: While motionless, the scarecrow is indistinguishable from
  an ordinary scarecrow.

Actions
Multiattack:
• The scarecrow makes two claw attacks.
Claw:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom
  save or become frightened until the end of the scarecrow's next turn.
Terrifying Glare:
• One creature within 30 feet that can see the scarecrow makes a DC 11 Wisdom
  save. On a failure it is frightened until the end of the scarecrow's next
  turn and paralyzed while frightened in this way.

Tactics:
Use this as the party's first clear combat tutorial. It begins alone, visibly
turns its head before initiative, and spends its first turn using Terrifying
Glare rather than Multiattack. Spread claw attacks between sturdy targets and
do not focus a paralyzed novice. It collapses when the chain link is removed,
giving the party a non-damage solution after they identify the link.
