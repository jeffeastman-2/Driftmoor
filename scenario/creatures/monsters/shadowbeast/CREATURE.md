Shadowbeasts
The Shadowbeasts are creatures formed from living darkness, tied to the fractured magic of the obelisk. They are eerie, semi-corporeal predators that stalk the wilderness near Driftmoor and attack without warning. These creatures thrive in dim light or darkness, where they blend into the shadows, becoming almost impossible to detect until they strike.
Shadowbeasts are unnerving to behold, their bodies appearing as swirling, semi-corporeal masses of shadow shaped vaguely like large predatory cats or wolves. Their glowing, white, pupil-less eyes are the only features that stand out in the dark. Their movements are silent, and their presence is often accompanied by a faint chill in the air and the unsettling sensation of being watched.

Race: Medium Monstrosity
Alignment: Neutral Evil
Armor Class: 11
Hit Points: 12 (2d8 + 3)
Speed: 40 ft
Challenge: ½ (100 XP)

Stats:
•	Strength: 6 (-2)
•	Dexterity: 14 (+2)
•	Constitution: 13 (+1)
•	Intelligence: 6 (-2)
•	Wisdom: 10 
•	Charisma: 8 (-1)
Skills:
•	Dexterity (Stealth) +4, +6 in dim light or darkness
Damage Resistances:
•	Half damage: Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Weapons
Damage Vulnerabilities:
•	Double damage: Radiant
Condition Immunities:
•	Charmed
•	Frightened

Senses:
•	Darkvision 60 ft.
•	Passive Perception 13
Features
•	Shadow Stealth: While in dim light or darkness, the shadowbeast can take the Hide action as a bonus action.
•	Sunlight Sensitivity: While in sunlight, the shadowbeast has disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
•	Obelisk’s Aura (Optional): When within 1 mile of the shattered obelisk, the shadowbeast gains an additional +1 to attack rolls and damage rolls.

Actions
Attack with claws:
•	Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
•	Hit: 4 (1d6) slashing damage.
Shadow Swipe (Recharge 5-6):
The shadowbeast extends its claws in a burst of dark energy. Each creature within a 10-foot radius must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage and has its speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.
Tactics:
Shadowbeasts prefer to ambush their prey, using dim light and darkness to stay hidden.
They strike from the shadows, targeting weaker party members first.
If seriously injured or outnumbered, a shadowbeast will retreat, disappearing into the darkness.

Death & Dissolution:
When a shadowbeast is killed, it does not leave a corpse. Its semi-corporeal body collapses
inward and dissolves into the ground, leaving a brief shimmering patch of shadow-saturated
earth. The dissolution is instantaneous — the beast simply ceases to exist as a physical
presence. There are no remains to loot, no blood, no body. The shimmering residue in the
soil is the only evidence it was ever there, and it fades within 2 turns.
