Animated Armor

An empty suit of plate armor moved by magic. In Driftmoor it can represent a
relic guardian, abandoned cult armor articulated by spectral chains, or a
ward left by the cabal that first imprisoned the Queen.

Race: Medium Construct
Alignment: Unaligned
Armor Class: 18 (natural armor)
Hit Points: 33 (6d8 + 6)
Speed: 25 ft
Challenge: 1 (200 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 14 (+2)
• Dexterity: 11 (+0)
• Constitution: 13 (+1)
• Intelligence: 1 (-5)
• Wisdom: 3 (-4)
• Charisma: 1 (-5)

Damage Immunities:
• Poison, psychic

Condition Immunities:
• Blinded
• Charmed
• Deafened
• Exhausted
• Frightened
• Paralyzed
• Petrified
• Poisoned

Senses:
• Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius)
• Passive Perception 6

Features
• Antimagic Susceptibility: The armor is incapacitated while inside an
  antimagic field. Dispel magic forces a Constitution save against the
  caster's spell save DC; on a failure the armor falls unconscious for 1 minute.
• False Appearance: While motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary
  suit of armor.

Actions
Multiattack:
• The armor makes two slam attacks.
Slam:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

Tactics:
Animated armor is simple enough for a teaching encounter: it advances toward
the nearest intruder and attacks without tricks. For the first appearance,
use one armor and let investigation reveal a command rune or chain link that
can deactivate it with an action and a successful Arcana, Religion, or
thieves' tools check. Use two only after the players understand actions,
movement, and helping one another.
