Horse

A horse is a large, domesticated herd animal bred for riding, hauling, and
farm work. Though usually gentle when accustomed to people, it is powerful,
quick to startle, and capable of defending itself with a forceful kick.

Race: Large Beast
Alignment: Unaligned
Armor Class: 10
Hit Points: 13 (2d10 + 2)
Speed: 60 ft
Challenge: 1/4 (50 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 16 (+3)
• Dexterity: 10 (+0)
• Constitution: 12 (+1)
• Intelligence: 2 (-4)
• Wisdom: 11 (+0)
• Charisma: 7 (-2)

Senses:
• Passive Perception 10

Features
• Herd Animal: A horse looks to familiar horses and trusted handlers for
  reassurance. Animal Handling checks made to calm it have advantage while a
  calm herd mate or trusted handler is nearby.
• Beast of Burden: The horse counts as one size larger when determining its
  carrying capacity and the weight it can push, drag, or lift.

Actions
Hooves:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

Tactics:
A domestic horse avoids combat. When frightened, it tries to retreat toward
its stall, herd, or trusted handler. If cornered, restrained, or protecting a
foal, it kicks once and then attempts to flee. It never pursues an attacker.

Death:
A killed horse leaves an ordinary animal carcass. Tack and horseshoes remain
separate, recoverable items.
