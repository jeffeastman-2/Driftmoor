Scout

Scouts are skilled hunters and trackers who offer their services for a fee. Most hunt wild game, but a few work as bounty hunters, serve as guides, or provide military reconnaissance.

Race: Medium Humanoid (any race)
Alignment: Any alignment
Armor Class: 13 (leather armor)
Hit Points: 16 (3d8+3)
Speed: 30 ft
Challenge: 1/2 (100 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 11 (+0)
• Dexterity: 14 (+2)
• Constitution: 12 (+1)
• Intelligence: 11 (+0)
• Wisdom: 13 (+1)
• Charisma: 11 (+0)

Skills:
• Nature +4, Perception +5, Stealth +6, Survival +5

Senses:
• Passive Perception 15

Features
• Keen Hearing and Sight: The scout has advantage on Wisdom (Perception)
  checks that rely on hearing or sight.

Actions
Multiattack:
• The scout makes two melee attacks or two ranged attacks.
Shortsword:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
Longbow:
• Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target.
• Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.

Tactics:
The scout observes before acting, attacks from concealment and range, and
relocates when discovered. Its first duty is to carry information back, not
to hold ground. When working with bandits, the scout identifies danger and
withdrawal routes rather than commanding the gang.
