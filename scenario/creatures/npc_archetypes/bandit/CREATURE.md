Bandit

Bandits roam in gangs and are led by a bandit captain or sometimes a thug,
veteran, or spellcaster. Not all bandits are evil. Oppression, drought,
disease, or famine can drive otherwise honest folk into banditry.

Race: Medium Humanoid (any race)
Alignment: Any non-good alignment
Armor Class: 12 (leather armor)
Hit Points: 11 (2d8 + 2)
Speed: 30 ft
Challenge: 1/8 (25 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 11 (+0)
• Dexterity: 12 (+1)
• Constitution: 12 (+1)
• Intelligence: 10 (+0)
• Wisdom: 10 (+0)
• Charisma: 10 (+0)

Senses:
• Passive Perception 10

Actions
Scimitar:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing damage.
Light Crossbow:
• Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target.
• Hit: 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

Tactics:
Bandits prefer favorable numbers, cover, and a visible escape route. They use
crossbows while targets approach, then draw scimitars only when necessary.
Most value survival over loot: a bandit retreats, surrenders, or bargains when
the captain falls, half the gang is defeated, or escape becomes possible.
