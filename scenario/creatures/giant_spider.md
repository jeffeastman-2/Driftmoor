Giant Spider

The Giant Spider is a large, predatory arachnid that lairs in caves, ruins,
and other dark places. It is the size of a large dog with eight thick,
bristled legs and chitinous black armor flecked with grey. Its eight eyes
catch torchlight as a constellation of small reflections. It hunts by ambush,
dropping on prey from above or webbing them and dragging them into a cocoon
to feed on later.

Race: Large Beast
Alignment: Unaligned
Armor Class: 14 (natural armor)
Hit Points: 26 (4d10 + 4)
Speed: 30 ft, climb 30 ft
Challenge: 1 (200 XP)

Stats:
•	Strength: 14 (+2)
•	Dexterity: 16 (+3)
•	Constitution: 12 (+1)
•	Intelligence: 2 (-4)
•	Wisdom: 11 (+0)
•	Charisma: 4 (-3)

Skills:
•	Stealth +7

Senses:
•	Blindsight 10 ft.
•	Darkvision 60 ft.
•	Passive Perception 10

Features
•	Spider Climb: The spider can climb difficult surfaces, including upside
    down on ceilings, without needing an ability check.
•	Web Sense: While in contact with a web, the spider knows the exact
    location of any other creature in contact with the same web.
•	Web Walker: The spider ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

Actions
Bite:
•	Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
•	Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 11
    Constitution saving throw, taking 9 (2d8) poison damage on a failed
    save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the poison damage
    reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but poisoned
    for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is paralyzed while
    poisoned in this way.

Web (Recharge 5-6):
•	Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one Large or smaller
    creature.
•	Hit: The target is restrained by webbing. As an action, the restrained
    target can make a DC 12 Strength check, bursting the webbing on a
    success. The webbing can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 10; HP 5;
    vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to bludgeoning, poison, and
    psychic damage).

Tactics:
The giant spider in the Echoing Caverns has lived undisturbed for years. It
hunts by ambush — clinging to the ceiling of the Secret Chamber where its
dark chitin disappears into shadow, then dropping onto the first creature
to step inside. It prefers to web isolated targets and drag them into a
cocoon for later feeding. If the prey is well-armed and well-lit, the
spider will retreat to the upper webs and harass with web-shots from
above before disengaging entirely.

Death:
A killed giant spider leaves a body — a heavy, chitinous corpse that
rapidly leaks pale ichor as the legs curl inward. The chitin can be cut
free for crafting (light armor scales, a shield boss). The fangs hold a
small reservoir of poison, sufficient for one or two doses if extracted
carefully within the hour.
