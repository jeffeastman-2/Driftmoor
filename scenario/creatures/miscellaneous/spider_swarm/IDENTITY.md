Spider Swarm.
- Swarm Body: hundreds of newly hatched spiders move and attack as one mass.
- Spider Climb: can climb walls and ceilings freely.
- Web Sense: knows the exact location of any creature touching the same web.
- Web Walker: ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.
- Bite: +3, 4d4 piercing while above half hit points, or 2d4 piercing while at or below half hit points; the target then makes a DC 11 Constitution save against 2d8 poison damage, taking half on a success.
- Paralytic Venom: a creature reduced to 0 hit points by the poison is stable, poisoned for 1 hour, and paralyzed while poisoned this way.
- Web (Recharge 5-6): ranged +5 to hit, 30/60 ft.; a Large or smaller target is restrained. The target can use an action to make a DC 12 Strength check to escape.
- Webbing: AC 10, 5 hit points, vulnerable to fire, and immune to bludgeoning, poison, and psychic damage.
- Brood Instinct: drops from ceilings or pours from ruptured egg sacs, surrounds exposed prey, and scatters into cracks when badly depleted.
