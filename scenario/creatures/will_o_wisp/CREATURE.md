Will-o'-Wisp

A malicious mote of cold light that drifts among the trees of the Fey Glade.
Here its glow shifts between moon-white and violet, mimicking the Pool's
discarded futures and tempting travelers away from safe paths.

Race: Tiny Undead
Alignment: Chaotic Evil
Armor Class: 19
Hit Points: 22 (9d4)
Speed: 0 ft, fly 50 ft (hover)
Challenge: 2 (450 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 1 (-5)
• Dexterity: 28 (+9)
• Constitution: 10 (+0)
• Intelligence: 13 (+1)
• Wisdom: 14 (+2)
• Charisma: 11 (+0)

Damage Resistances:
• Acid, cold, fire, necrotic, thunder
• Bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

Damage Immunities:
• Lightning, poison

Condition Immunities:
• Exhausted
• Grappled
• Paralyzed
• Poisoned
• Prone
• Restrained
• Unconscious

Senses:
• Darkvision 120 ft.
• Passive Perception 12

Features
• Ephemeral: The wisp cannot wear or carry anything.
• Incorporeal Movement: It can move through creatures and objects as difficult
  terrain, taking 5 force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
• Variable Illumination: It sheds bright light from 5 to 20 feet and matching
  dim light beyond that; it can alter or extinguish this light as a bonus action.

Actions
Shock:
• Melee Spell Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature.
• Hit: 9 (2d8) lightning damage.
Invisibility:
• The wisp and its light become invisible until it attacks, uses Consume Life,
  or ends invisibility with concentration.
Consume Life:
• Bonus action against one creature at 0 hit points within 5 feet. The target
  makes a DC 10 Constitution save; on a failure it dies and the wisp heals 10
  hit points.

Tactics:
For the party's first encounters, the Glade's wisps test rather than execute.
Use one wisp at a time, keep it visible for the opening round, and omit Consume
Life. It shocks once, leads the party toward a hazard or revelation, and flees
when reduced below half hit points. Add a second wisp only after the players
understand movement, readied attacks, and magical defenses.
