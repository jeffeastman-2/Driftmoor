Drow

A lightly armed citizen-soldier of the buried drow city, preserved for centuries
inside a translucent cocoon of temporal webbing.

Race: Medium Humanoid (Elf)
Alignment: Neutral Evil
Armor Class: 15 (chain shirt)
Hit Points: 13 (3d8)
Speed: 30 ft
Challenge: 1/4 (50 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 10 (+0)
• Dexterity: 14 (+2)
• Constitution: 10 (+0)
• Intelligence: 11 (+0)
• Wisdom: 11 (+0)
• Charisma: 12 (+1)

Skills:
• Perception +2
• Stealth +4

Senses:
• Darkvision 120 ft.
• Passive Perception 12

Features
• Fey Ancestry: The drow has advantage on saves against being charmed, and
  magic cannot put it to sleep.
• Sunlight Sensitivity: In direct sunlight, the drow has disadvantage on
  attack rolls and on sight-based Wisdom (Perception) checks.
• Innate Spellcasting: Charisma is the spellcasting ability (save DC 11).
  The drow can cast dancing lights at will and faerie fire once per day.
• Web-Sleep Disorientation: During its first round after awakening from an
  ancient cocoon, the drow cannot take reactions and has disadvantage on its
  first attack roll.

Actions
Shortsword:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
Hand Crossbow:
• Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.
• Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 11
  Constitution save or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

Tactics:
On waking, the drow seeks context before committing to a fight. It calls out
in Elvish or Undercommon, protects nearby sleepers, and attacks anyone harming
the cocoons. If combat begins, it marks intruders with faerie fire and fights
from cover. It surrenders or retreats when isolated rather than dying for a
queen whose present condition it does not yet understand.
