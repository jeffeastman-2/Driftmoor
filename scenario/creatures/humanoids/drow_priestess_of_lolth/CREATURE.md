Drow Priestess of Lolth

An ancient drow queen and high priestess held upon a black bier by six intact
fey chains and one broken chain. Vaelithra can influence the crypt but cannot
leave it while the binding persists.

Race: Medium Humanoid (Elf)
Alignment: Neutral Evil
Armor Class: 16 (scale mail)
Hit Points: 71 (13d8 + 13)
Speed: 30 ft
Challenge: 8 (3,900 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 10 (+0)
• Dexterity: 14 (+2)
• Constitution: 12 (+1)
• Intelligence: 13 (+1)
• Wisdom: 17 (+3)
• Charisma: 18 (+4)

Skills:
• Insight +6
• Perception +6
• Religion +4
• Stealth +5

Senses:
• Darkvision 120 ft.
• Passive Perception 16

Features
• Fey Ancestry: The priestess has advantage on saves against being charmed,
  and magic cannot put her to sleep.
• Sunlight Sensitivity: In direct sunlight, the priestess has disadvantage on
  attack rolls and on sight-based Wisdom (Perception) checks.
• Innate Spellcasting: Charisma is the spellcasting ability (save DC 15).
  She can cast dancing lights at will, and faerie fire and darkness once per day.
• Spellcasting: The priestess is a 10th-level Wisdom spellcaster (save DC 14,
  +6 to hit). Typical magic includes guidance, resistance, thaumaturgy;
  command, cure wounds, guiding bolt; hold person, silence, spiritual weapon;
  dispel magic, spirit guardians; banishment; and insect plague.
• Crypt-Bound: While any four fey chains remain anchored, the priestess cannot
  willingly leave the bier, make weapon attacks, or target a creature outside
  the crypt. She can speak telepathically to creatures touched by the prison's
  magic and can use the Crypt Actions below.
• Queen's Guile: The priestess has advantage on Charisma checks made while
  offering a creature a truthful possible future.

Actions
Multiattack:
• If freed from Crypt-Bound, the priestess makes two scourge attacks.
Scourge:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage plus 17 (5d6) poison damage.

Crypt Actions
On initiative count 20, while Crypt-Bound, the priestess uses one effect:
• Whispered Future: One creature that can hear her must succeed on a DC 14
  Wisdom save or be charmed until the end of its next turn. The effect ends
  early if an ally uses an action to remind it what is presently real.
• Lash of the Seventh Chain: One creature within 60 feet must succeed on a
  DC 14 Dexterity save or take 7 (2d6) force damage and be pulled 10 feet.
• Wake the Court: One nearby web-sleep cocoon begins opening. It opens at the
  next initiative count 20 unless a creature uses an action to stabilize its
  runes with Arcana, Religion, thieves' tools, or another credible method.

Tactics:
Vaelithra's objective is to make the party turn the key toward release. She
uses truthful temptations, contradictory accounts of the ancient war, and
pressure against ritual stations. She does not trade ineffectual attacks with
the party while entombed. If freed, she prioritizes escape and awakening her
court over killing the adventurers. The intended resolution is rebinding,
negotiation, or a consequential choice—not reducing her hit points to zero.
