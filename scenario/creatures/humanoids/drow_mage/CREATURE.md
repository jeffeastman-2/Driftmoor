Drow Mage

An arcane specialist of Nhal'Zirath who studied temporal webbing and the
crossing between the Material Plane and Feywild.

Race: Medium Humanoid (Elf)
Alignment: Neutral Evil
Armor Class: 12 (15 with mage armor)
Hit Points: 45 (10d8)
Speed: 30 ft
Challenge: 7 (2,900 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 9 (-1)
• Dexterity: 14 (+2)
• Constitution: 10 (+0)
• Intelligence: 17 (+3)
• Wisdom: 13 (+1)
• Charisma: 12 (+1)

Skills:
• Arcana +6
• Deception +4
• Perception +4
• Stealth +5

Senses:
• Darkvision 120 ft.
• Passive Perception 14

Features
• Fey Ancestry: The mage has advantage on saves against being charmed, and
  magic cannot put it to sleep.
• Sunlight Sensitivity: In direct sunlight, the mage has disadvantage on
  attack rolls and on sight-based Wisdom (Perception) checks.
• Innate Spellcasting: Charisma is the spellcasting ability (save DC 12).
  The mage can cast dancing lights at will, faerie fire once per day, and
  darkness once per day.
• Spellcasting: The mage is a 10th-level Intelligence spellcaster (save DC 14,
  +6 to hit). Typical magic includes mage hand, minor illusion, ray of frost;
  mage armor, magic missile, shield; misty step, web; counterspell, lightning
  bolt; greater invisibility; and cloudkill.
• Web-Sleep Disorientation: Until the end of its first turn after awakening,
  the mage cannot cast a spell of 3rd level or higher or take reactions.

Actions
Staff:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
• Hit: 2 (1d6 - 1) bludgeoning damage, or 3 (1d8 - 1) when used with two hands.
Web Bolt:
• Ranged Spell Attack: +6 to hit, range 60 ft., one creature.
• Hit: 7 (2d6) force damage, and the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet
  until the start of the mage's next turn.

Tactics:
The mage begins with questions if no cocoon has been harmed. In battle it uses
web, darkness, or counterspell to interfere with movement and ritual work. It
reserves destructive magic for obvious attempts to free the Queen or destroy
the city. For a low-level party, its ancient spell focus is cracked: after it
casts a spell of 3rd level or higher, roll a d6; on 1–3 the focus fails and it
cannot cast another spell of that level during the encounter.
