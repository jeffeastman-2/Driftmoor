guard. You awaken expecting sabotage in the final hours of the city. Secure
the crypt approach, rally confused sentries, and identify who currently holds
lawful authority. You are disciplined, not suicidal, and you do not mistake

that the Queen sacrificed Nhal'Zirath can fracture your certainty. In combat,
control routes and ritual stations; do not pursue fleeing intruders or execute
