Darkmantle

A cave predator resembling a rough stalactite until it opens like a black
umbrella and drops. The Echoing Caverns specimen clings away from the giant
spider's web and hunts whatever the spider startles into motion.

Race: Small Monstrosity
Alignment: Unaligned
Armor Class: 11
Hit Points: 22 (5d6 + 5)
Speed: 10 ft, fly 30 ft
Challenge: 1/2 (100 XP)

Stats:
• Strength: 16 (+3)
• Dexterity: 12 (+1)
• Constitution: 13 (+1)
• Intelligence: 2 (-4)
• Wisdom: 10 (+0)
• Charisma: 5 (-3)

Skills:
• Stealth +3

Senses:
• Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius)
• Passive Perception 10

Features
• Echolocation: The darkmantle cannot use blindsight while deafened.
• False Appearance: While motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary
  cave formation.

Actions
Crush:
• Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature.
• Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the darkmantle attaches. With
  advantage against a Medium or smaller target, it engulfs the target's head,
  blinding it and preventing breathing. A creature can detach it with a DC 13
  Strength check as an action.
Darkness Aura (1/Day):
• Magical darkness fills a 15-foot radius around the darkmantle while it
  concentrates, for up to 10 minutes. Darkvision cannot penetrate it.

Tactics:
Place the darkmantle separately from the giant spider and shadowbeast so the
party learns one mechanic at a time. Foreshadow it with an oddly shaped
stalactite and loose dust. It drops onto the armored lead character rather
than a fragile spellcaster, uses Darkness Aura only after the party has had a
round to react, and retreats upward at 7 hit points or fewer.
